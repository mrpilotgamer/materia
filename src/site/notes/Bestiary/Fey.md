The fey are native to [[Places/Materia\|Materia]]. The world is their birthplace and their only home, and they keep to its wild places: deep forest, open sea, high peaks and the ground beneath the ground. They are as varied as the mortal peoples. Some are small enough to sleep in a flower, some stand taller than the oldest trees, and no two kinds keep quite the same habits.

## The Seven Courts

The [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials\|Primordials]] are lords over all fey. Some fey also belong to a court, one of seven gatherings under a single Primordial, and many belong to none. A court changes whatever kind of fey joins it, so a dryad of the White Court is a winter dryad, and a pixie of the Red Court carries fire. The courts have no thrones or written laws.

- **[[The Red Court\|The Red Court]]** - fey of fire, upheaval and autumn, under [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Aka, Red Primordial Spirit\|Aka]]
- **[[The Blue Court\|The Blue Court]]** - fey of the seas, rivers and rain, under [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Aoi, Blue Primordial Spirit\|Aoi]]
- **[[The Yellow Court\|The Yellow Court]]** - fey of sunlight and summer warmth, under [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Ki, Yellow Primordial Spirit\|Ki]]
- **[[The Black Court\|The Black Court]]** - fey of the night sky, the undergrowth and rot, under [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Kuro, Black Primordial Spirit\|Kuro]]
- **[[The Green Court\|The Green Court]]** - fey of living things, forests and spring, under [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Midori, Green Primordial Spirit\|Midori]]
- **[[The Purple Court\|The Purple Court]]** - fey of magic, caves and the unknown, under [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Murasakino, Purple Primordial Spirit\|Murasakino]]
- **[[The White Court\|The White Court]]** - fey of winter, wind and ice, under [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Shiroi, White Primordial Spirit\|Shiroi]]

## The Fey and the Mortal Peoples

The fey and the settled lands keep a balance. Fey rarely come into towns, and most mortals leave the deep wilds alone. When the empires of the [[History/Ages/Age of Expansion\|Age of Expansion]] tipped that balance, the fey rose with the Primordials in the [[History/Ages/Age of Incursion\|Age of Incursion]] and brought cities down alongside them. The balance has mostly held since.

## Fey Peoples

The [[Species and Cultures/Races/Chisana\|Chisana]] are a fey people who live among the mortal races, and most keep close ties to the Primordials. Some believe the [[Species and Cultures/Races/Mannee\|Mannee]] are related to the fey as well, though nobody has proven it.
