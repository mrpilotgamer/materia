## Physical Description

Powerfully built and imposing, the Trox are insect-like humanoids of large stature who tower over most other races. They walk upright on two strong, chitinous legs, possess two primary arms capable of wielding weapons and performing heavy labor, and feature four smaller vestigial arms extending from their midsection that are used mainly for manipulating small objects. Their exoskeleton provides natural protection, and their clawed hands are formidable natural weapons.

## Biology

The Trox are built for life underground. They see in the dark out to sixty feet, and they have an instinct for caves and for surviving where there is no light. Their shells are natural armor, and their extra arms let them do several things at once, though the small arms are weak. Their bodies are something between a humanoid's and a monster's, which gives them a few resistances and abilities humanoids don't have. For all their claws and frightening looks, Trox think and feel as deeply as anyone else.

## Origins

The Trox come from the deep caves of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]], which were their ancestral homes. Whether they grew up down there in the dark or came about some other way in [[History/Ages/Prehistory\|Prehistory]] is an open question. They probably split off from the other humanoid peoples very early and changed as they adapted to life underground. Their move to the surface, and their part in making the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Trow\|Trow]] culture, is recent next to their long history. [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Alfugel, God of Hatred\|Alfugel, God of Hatred]] is said to take the form of a Trox, and Trox communities hold Alfugel in particular reverence. Nobody knows whether that bond started before or after they came up into the light.

## Where They Live

The Trox came from the caves beneath [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]]. The largest Trox population today is in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/The Rayhostian Theocracy\|The Rayhostian Theocracy]], and the Trox live by [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Trow\|Trow]] culture on both shores of the strait.
