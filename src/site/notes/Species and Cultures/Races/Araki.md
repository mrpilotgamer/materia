## Physical Description

The Araki are tall and heavily muscled, and they look fierce. They move with a grace and precision nobody expects from them, and they tend to be better at work that takes a light touch than work that takes brute strength.

## Biology

The Araki possess a biological trait known as Arakian Rebirth. When an Araki dies and their body remains undisturbed, they spontaneously revive one month later as a young adult, stripped of nearly all memories of their previous life. This regeneration fails only if the brain is destroyed during the waiting period. Araki also possess darkvision extending to 120 feet and demonstrate unusual tenacity in combat, capable of continuing to fight even after sustaining mortal wounds.

## Origins

Nobody knows where the Araki came from. Coming back from the dead looks like the work of a god or of very old magic, perhaps something tied to souls or rebirth from before the [[History/Great Schism War\|Great Schism War]]. Some think their ancestors lived through a catastrophe that changed them and left them this way of surviving. The Araki themselves point to the "shards of the past," the scraps of knowledge each one carries over from earlier lives, as a sign that their people are older than any written record, built on more deaths and rebirths than anyone could count.

The Araki's homeland is [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Yoichizu\|Yoichizu]], in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Azure Expanse\|The Azure Expanse]], where the great majority live by [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Aj'Snaga\|Aj'Snaga]] culture. The Aj'Snaga treat the reborn as reincarnations: a new person with a new given name, keeping the clan name but none of the old standing, debts, or grudges.

Family or close friends keep the *tsuya*, a month-long vigil over the body, and name the reborn when they wake. The shards of the past are honored as all that remains of the previous person. Destroying the brain during the vigil is the gravest taboo, permitted only as *shūen*, the true death, the final punishment for one who has wronged their people.

Not every people sees rebirth as a blessing. Many followers of [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Kuro, Black Primordial Spirit\|Kuro]] regard the Araki's escape from death as unnatural, and a few rank them with liches and other undead, a comparison the Aj'Snaga resent bitterly.

## Where They Live

The homeland of the Araki is [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Yoichizu\|Yoichizu]], in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Azure Expanse\|The Azure Expanse]], where most of them live by [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Aj'Snaga\|Aj'Snaga]] culture. Many also live among the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Portizian\|Portizian]] peoples of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imestalia\|Imestalia]] and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Pimestalia\|Pimestalia]].
