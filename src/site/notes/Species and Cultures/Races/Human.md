## Physical Description

Humans have nothing special about their bodies: no scales, no thick hide, no natural armor. They're middling in build and plain to look at, and the most notable thing about them is how much they vary. What they lack in natural defenses, they make up for in stubbornness and adaptability.

## Biology

For all that they look fragile, humans are about as hard to kill as the Drakmed. They simply refuse to give up when others would. They pick up new skills faster than most races, and their curiosity sends them looking for new answers to old problems. Their lives are shorter than many races', and some think that's why they're always in such a hurry.

## Origins

Nobody knows where humans came from. Other races can point to dragon blood, fey blood or the lower planes. Humans seem to have simply appeared during [[History/Ages/Prehistory\|Prehistory]], with no magic or god behind them that anyone can find. One idea is that they grew up alongside the world after the [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials\|Primordials]] shaped it, getting clever because everything else was bigger. Another is that humans are just what a people turns into when it has to adapt or die. They live almost everywhere on [[Places/Materia\|Materia]] and fit into almost any culture, so they've probably been around since the earliest days.

Some historians believe the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Portizian\|Portizian]] culture began among humans on the coast around Quichollow, in what is now [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imestalia\|Imestalia]], and that those humans came from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]], though no one has proven it.

## Where They Live

Humans live almost everywhere in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Alora\|Alora]]. They rule [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/The Rayhostian Theocracy\|The Rayhostian Theocracy]] by [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Anor\|Anor]] culture, make up about two-fifths of the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Kingdom of Stamvia\|Kingdom of Stamvia]], and are common across [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]] and among the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Portizian\|Portizian]] peoples of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Azure Expanse\|The Azure Expanse]].
