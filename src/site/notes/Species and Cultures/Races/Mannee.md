## Physical Description

The Mannee are small, gentle, bird-like people covered in feathers from head to foot. In their own shape they look like small feathered humans, slightly built, with sharp bird's eyes. They're light enough that their wing-like arms let them glide, though they can't truly fly.

## Biology

Two things set the Mannee apart. First, they have no sexes, and they can change their appearance at will to look like almost any other kind of humanoid, though the change doesn't give them any new abilities. Second, every Mannee can work magic, even those who were never taught, so the magic seems to be part of their bodies. In their own feathered shape they can't be hurt by a fall and can glide, but they lose both when they take another form. Their senses, and their eyesight most of all, are sharper than most humanoids'.

## Origins

Nobody knows how the Mannee began in [[History/Ages/Prehistory\|Prehistory]], and their shapeshifting and lack of sexes have fed a lot of guesses. The most common is that someone made them, perhaps as servants or messengers who needed to pass among every kind of people. Others think they're related to the [[Bestiary/Fey\|Fey]], given how magical and changeable they are, though nobody has proven it. A few believe the Mannee changed themselves long ago, trading strength and a fixed shape for magic and the ability to fit in anywhere. If they were made, whoever made them, god, mage or [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials\|Primordials]], had a purpose that has long been forgotten.

The Mannee's homeland is [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imnas\|Imnas]], in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Azure Expanse\|The Azure Expanse]], where the great majority live by [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Dulandi\|Dulandi]] culture. Because every Mannee works magic, magic among the Dulandi is as ordinary as farming, and standing among them comes from magical skill.
