## Physical Description

The Vullukk are lean, nimble climbers. Their bodies are made for going up: they scale cliffs and pick their way along mountain paths that would stop most other races.

## Biology

Vullukk climb easily, keep their balance on the worst footing, and don't suffer in thin mountain air that would leave others gasping. They see in the dark out to 120 feet, and their reflexes are quick enough that they sometimes dodge a blow nobody should be able to dodge. Most of all, they're curious. A Vullukk can't help collecting facts, trivia and odd bits of knowledge about the world, and they seem to hold onto what they learn better than most.

## Origins

Bodies built for climbing and thin air say the Vullukk came from the mountains, sometime in [[History/Ages/Prehistory\|Prehistory]], though nobody agrees which mountains. Their curiosity may have started as a way to survive, since in hard mountain country the ones who paid attention and remembered things lived longer. Some think the curiosity runs deeper than that, and that something which prized knowledge made the Vullukk that way on purpose. Their habit of collecting facts "one at a time" is older than any written record, and every Vullukk adds what they learn to what their people know.

Some [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Valekai\|Valekai]] claim the mountain range of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]] as the place the Vullukk first climbed into the world.

## Where They Live

Vullukk are common across [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]], especially in the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Empire of Nox]], the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Republic of Korthak\|Republic of Korthak]] and the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Cilomutesan Union\|Cilomutesan Union]]. The mountain clans of the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Valekai\|Valekai]] are almost purely Vullukk.
