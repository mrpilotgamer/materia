## Physical Description

The Drakmed are tough and solidly built, and their dragon blood shows: patches of scales, reptile eyes and other dragonish features. They can take a beating that would put most races down.

## Biology

What sets the Drakmed apart is the *Draconis Fundamentum*, a small version of the gland a true dragon breathes with. It lets a Drakmed breathe out a cone of acid, cold, lightning or fire, though the strain means they can only do it once a day. Magic and nature treat a Drakmed as both humanoid and dragon, and their dragon blood makes them unusually hard to hurt.

## Origins

The Drakmed come from true dragons, though exactly how is unclear. [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]] of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Alora\|Alora]] are widely believed to be where dragons began, and the first Drakmed probably appeared there too, maybe in [[History/Ages/Prehistory\|Prehistory]], when dragons mixed more freely with other peoples. Whether they came from dragons taking mortal partners, from magic, or simply from living near dragons for long enough, nobody can say. Some old texts claim the dragons made or blessed certain bloodlines on purpose, but if so, nobody remembers why.

## Where They Live

Most of the people of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Narathatath\|Narathatath]], in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]], are Drakmed, and they live by [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Drako\|Drako]] culture.
