## Physical Description

The Yol-Mar are powerfully built humanoids bearing the marks of the fire giant ancestry they claim, taller and broader than typical humans, with skin that seems to shimmer with inner heat. Their passionate nature manifests in physical presence as much as in temperament, and they carry themselves with the confidence of people who believe themselves descended from titans. Whatever their heritage, they stand at humanoid scale and never at the scale of true giants.

## Biology

The fire within the Yol-Mar is real, whatever its source, and anyone can witness it. Their weapons, whether natural attacks, unarmed strikes, or wielded arms, ignite with supernatural heat upon striking, dealing searing fire damage alongside physical trauma. Their skin possesses innate resistance to flame and heat that would burn other races, a gift the Yol-Mar credit to their giant ancestors. This fire resistance and the ability to channel flame through their strikes mark them as creatures whose very biology incorporates elemental power. They also take naturally to the massive weapons of the giant sagas, greatswords and earthbreakers wielded with ancestral skill.

## Origins

The Yol-Mar claim descent from the ancient fire giants of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Jotuun Nexus\|The Jotuun Nexus]], known as the "Birth and Deathplace of Giants." No one has proven the claim, since whatever evidence once existed was lost over the millennia, but it is widely believed, and the Yol-Mar fire is the strongest argument for it.

Among those who accept it, how humanoid-scale Yol-Mar arose from true giants remains a matter of scholarly debate. Some theorize they emerged from unions between giants and humanoids during [[History/Ages/Prehistory\|Prehistory]], their bloodline stabilizing at medium size over generations while retaining elemental gifts. Others propose the Yol-Mar represent a deliberate transformation, perhaps giants who chose to walk among smaller races or were transformed by magical catastrophe during the [[History/Great Schism War\|Great Schism War]]. A third theory suggests the Yol-Mar may be what remains of a giant civilization that diminished in size but not in fire, their reduced stature a price paid for survival when true giants declined. Whatever their origin, the Yol-Mar maintain strong cultural connections to their claimed heritage and are known for a passionate intensity they credit to their forebears.

[[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Helano\|Helano]], the desert khanate at the heart of the Nexus, was the Yol-Mar homeland, and its Khan was always one of the strongest Yol-Mar in the land. When Helano fell to plague in 5267, its survivors scattered across the continent, most of them into [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Hovden\|Hovden]] and the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Kingdom of Stamvia\|Kingdom of Stamvia]], where Yol-Mar communities already stood. Roughly half of Stamvia's people are Yol-Mar today, and a few stubborn clans remain in the abandoned desert. Most Yol-Mar live by [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Yotuun\|Yotuun]] custom and make up a large share of Yotuun communities across the Nexus.
