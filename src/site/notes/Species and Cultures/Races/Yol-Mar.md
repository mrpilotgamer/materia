## Physical Description

The Yol-Mar are big, strong people, taller and broader than humans, with skin that seems to glow faintly with heat. They carry themselves like people who believe their ancestors were titans. Whatever their blood, they are human-sized, nowhere near as big as a true giant.

## Biology

The fire in the Yol-Mar is real, wherever it came from, and anyone can see it. Whatever they hit with, fist, claw or blade, flares with heat and burns as it strikes. Their skin shrugs off heat and flame that would burn anyone else, and the Yol-Mar credit their giant ancestors for it. They also have a natural gift for the huge weapons of the giant sagas, greatswords and earthbreakers.

## Origins

The Yol-Mar claim descent from the ancient fire giants of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Jotuun Nexus\|The Jotuun Nexus]], known as the "Birth and Deathplace of Giants." No one has proven the claim, since whatever evidence once existed was lost over the millennia, but it is widely believed, and the Yol-Mar fire is the strongest argument for it.

Those who believe it still argue over how giants became human-sized people. The usual answer is that giants and humanoids had children together in [[History/Ages/Prehistory\|Prehistory]], and over the generations the line settled at its present size and kept the fire. Others think some giants changed on purpose to live among smaller peoples, or were shrunk by some disaster in the [[History/Great Schism War\|Great Schism War]]. Either way, the Yol-Mar hold tightly to their giant heritage, and they put their fierce tempers down to their ancestors.

[[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Helano\|Helano]], the desert khanate at the heart of the Nexus, was the Yol-Mar homeland, and its Khan was always one of the strongest Yol-Mar in the land. When Helano fell to plague in 5267, its survivors scattered across the continent, most of them into [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Hovden\|Hovden]] and the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Kingdom of Stamvia\|Kingdom of Stamvia]], where Yol-Mar communities already stood. Roughly half of Stamvia's people are Yol-Mar today, and a few stubborn clans remain in the abandoned desert. Most Yol-Mar live by [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Jotuun\|Jotuun]] custom and make up a large share of Jotuun communities across the Nexus.

Yol-Mar also live beyond Materia. In Nidavellir, the cavern layer of [[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Heroic Domains of Ysgard\|Ysgard]], they share kingdoms with fire giants. Believers in the fire-giant ancestry call this proof. Skeptics say living together makes nobody kin.
