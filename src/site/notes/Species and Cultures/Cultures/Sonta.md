## Overview

Sonta culture is built around the city, the council hall, and the mask. Its people are merchants, shipwrights, and glassmakers who govern themselves as republics, trade across the northern sea, and guard their cities with a watchfulness born of 5267. They keep the legal codes of the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Eldar\|Eldar]] as the foundation of their law and hold that no one should wield power behind a hidden face.

Masks run through every part of Sonta life, and the rules for when to wear one and when to go barefaced are among the first things a Sontan child learns.

## Origins & Distribution

Sonta culture descends from the people of the Oligarchy of Presium, which held the centre of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]] before 5267. Presium was Eldar, but its coastal cities had long been a thing apart from the rest of the Eldar world. Shortly before the Faceless Century they were bound together into a maritime oligarchy, ruled by a closed circle of great merchant families who held its councils and its wealth among themselves and passed both down through their lines.

Presium fell with Adriacium in the [[History/Timeline\|war of 5267]]. The city-states that rose from its ruins fought one another for power in the years that followed. In the eastern half of the old oligarchy, the harbor cities that came through those wars turned away from rule by a few great families and built merchant republics in its place. Over the following decades they joined together as the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Cilomutesan Union\|Cilomutesan Union]], and their shared ways became Sonta culture.

Sonta is dominant across the Union from coast to coast and along the eastern edge of the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Republic of Korthak\|Republic of Korthak]]. The Union's territory takes in the northern end of the continent's mountain range, but the Sonta are a people of the coasts and river valleys who work the peaks for ore and stone and leave the high passes to the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Valekai\|Valekai]]. Those northern peaks were abandoned by the Valekai during the war of 5267, and Sonta miners moved into them in the years that followed.

The greatest Sonta cities are harbors, while market towns and river towns fill the interior between the two coasts. Sonta traders and shipwrights are found in ports across the northern sea.


[[Species and Cultures/Races/Human\|Humans]] and [[Species and Cultures/Races/Daemedi\|Daemedi]] make up the Sonta in roughly equal numbers. [[Species and Cultures/Races/Vullukk\|Vullukk]] are the next largest group, and members of other races are present in small numbers.

## Social Structure

The Sonta build society around the *ca'*, the house. A ca' is at once a family and a firm, made up of blood relations, spouses, apprentices, and partners who share its name, its trade, and its debts. The word also names the great house where the family lives and does its business, which in a harbor city usually stands on the water.

Each Sonta city governs itself as a republic. Its *Consegio*, the Great Council, is open to every householder who pays into the city, and it makes law, sets tariffs, and elects the city's officers. At the head of each city stands the *dose*, elected by the Consegio to lead it for a fixed term and bound by its laws.

Beneath the Consegio, working life is organized through the *scuole*, confraternities of trade and neighborhood. Each scuola keeps a hall, cares for its sick and widowed members, sets the standards of its craft, and marches under its own banner at festivals. Membership in a respected scuola matters almost as much to a Sontan as membership in a respected ca'.

The Sonta kept Eldar law and remade Eldar citizenship to suit a republic. Anyone who keeps a household in a city and pays into it may sit in its Consegio, whatever their ancestry. Newcomers who take a trade and join a scuola can earn that standing within a generation.

## Values & Beliefs

The Sonta prize three things above all. First comes the republic, which they call the *Serenità*, the calm and ordered life of a well-governed city. Honest dealing under the law comes next, since a contract witnessed and recorded binds as firmly as any oath. Last is vigilance, because every Sontan knows what came out of Adriacium in 5267 and what the thin barrier can still let through.

Presium's fall taught the Sonta to distrust power held by a few. Their republics spread office widely, limit its terms, and watch those who hold it. Any family that tries to buy the Consegio or pass an office down through its line is met with suspicion.

The Sonta teach that the governed may hide their faces but those who govern may not. Every Sontan knows the history of Adriacium's faceless emperor, who ruled for a century without ever showing his face, whatever they believe about who he truly was. A dose, a judge, or a captain of the watch goes barefaced for as long as they hold office. Anyone who exercises power over another in a mask commits the gravest breach of Sonta custom.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Malbing, God of Consumption\|Malbing]] is honored across the Wildlands, and the Sonta give them their due at harvest and at funerals. [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Aoi, Blue Primordial Spirit\|Aoi]] holds a special place as the power of the sea that carries their trade. [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Meregoma, God of the Ego\|Meregoma]], whose holy symbol is a face crowned with a golden laurel, is the patron of the mask, of the true self beneath it, and of the honest face shown when it counts.

Magic is a craft to the Sonta, respected most in shipwrights, glassmakers, and the watch. Magic that alters a face or disguises a voice is treated as a kind of mask and falls under the same rules. Any official caught wearing an illusion of a face is held to have broken the gravest rule of all.

## Daily Life & Customs

### Language

Sontan is descended from the Eldaran of Presium, and a Sontan who studies Eldar legal texts can follow them with effort. The two are no longer the same language. Sontan is softer and quicker in the ear, clipped at the ends of its words, and full of the vocabulary of ships, glass, and trade. Contracts and court records still borrow heavily from Classical Eldaran phrasing.

### Masks

Children go barefaced. At coming of age each Sontan receives a first mask, usually made to their own design, and with it the right to go masked in public. The mask marks them as a citizen who may keep their own counsel, bargain unseen, and speak their mind without their face betraying them.

Masks are common in the market, at the gaming table, at parties, and on the streets after dark. Votes in the Consegio are cast masked, so that no great ca' can see how a lesser neighbor voted and punish them for it. A masked person is addressed with the courtesy owed to any citizen, and asking who is beneath a mask is rude.

Some places require a bare face. Oaths, contracts, and testimony are given barefaced, and so is any plea before a judge. A Sontan unmasks at home, before the dead, and in the presence of a priest. Anyone holding office goes barefaced in public for their whole term, and a guest who keeps their mask on inside a house after being welcomed insults the host.

The watch may order any person to unmask when there is cause, since a mask is also the easiest disguise for a demon or for someone under demonic influence, and refusing the watch is a crime in itself.

### The Watch

Sonta military strength is built for defense. City walls, harbor chains, and a strong coastal fleet guard against raiders, and the cities keep no army for foreign campaigns. The most respected force in any Sonta city is the *Signori de Note*, the Lords of the Night, who keep the peace after dark and hunt demons and demonic influence within the walls.

The Lords of the Night carry on the Eldar tradition of the Wards of Binding, the magistrates who keep knowledge of planar entities. [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Bornumia]] keeps the Wards and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Nox]] the Phylakes. In the Sonta cities the office became the Lords of the Night, a working office and its captains answer to the Consegio. Many of the watch honor [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Dilliminner, God of Caution\|Dilliminner]], Lord of Preparations, alongside the gods of the city.

### Craft and Trade

The Sonta build ships. Each harbor city keeps its *arsenal*, a walled shipyard where scuole of carpenters, caulkers, rope-makers, and oar-makers work in sequence to turn out hulls at a pace other peoples find hard to believe. Naval vessels are among the Union's leading exports, along with the services of Sonta crews and naval instructors.

Glassmaking is the other great Sonta craft. The glassworks are set apart from the cities for fear of fire, and their makers turn out windows, lamps, mirrors, beads, and the fine painted glass used in masks. Glassmakers' secrets are guarded closely, and a master who takes them abroad is disgraced.

Fish, salt, and preserved foods round out the trade. Sonta merchants deal mainly in what their own cities make, and leave the long carrying trade across Alora to [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Laurland\|Laurland]].

### Life Events

A child's name is recorded in the city's book of births, and the ca' holds a feast in the child's honor. At sixteen each young Sontan receives their first mask before the household and the scuola, and is entered in the city's rolls as an adult.

Couples marry barefaced before witnesses, and the contract of marriage is read aloud and signed at the ceremony. The dead are buried barefaced, and their masks are hung in the hall of their scuola or kept by the family. An old ca' may keep the masks of many generations along one wall.

### Dress and Architecture

Sonta dress is suited to damp harbors and cold winters. Men and women alike wear the *tabaro*, a long, full cloak of heavy wool, over layered tunics and hose. Colors mark a person's scuola on festival days, and the wealthy wear velvet and brocade. The mask completes a Sontan's public dress, and fine masks of leather, lacquered paper, or painted glass are prized possessions.

In the harbor cities, the great houses rise straight from the water, with arched windows, carved balconies, and gates at water level where boats come and go. Canals cut through the harbor districts, and much of the city's traffic moves by boat.

Inland towns are built around a central square, where the council hall faces the scuola halls and the market. Their streets are lined with covered arcades that keep walkers out of the rain and snow, and the houses are of stone and timber with steep roofs against the long winters. Rivers carry the interior's goods down to the harbors, and a river town's wharves are as busy as its square. In town and harbor alike, colored glass fills the windows of wealthy houses and scuola halls.

### Festivals

The greatest festival is the *Carneval*, a season of feasting and masking in the depths of winter. For its length the rules loosen, and even a dose may go masked among the crowds. Its last night ends at midnight, when the whole city unmasks together in the square.

Each spring, when the ships make ready for the sailing season, the Sonta hold the Marriage of the Sea. The dose rows out into the harbor at the head of a procession of boats and casts a ring into the water, wedding the city to Aoi and asking the sea's goodwill for the year's voyages. Inland towns cast their ring into a river that runs to the sea, trusting the current to carry it to Aoi.

## Cultural Identity

A Sontan is known first by their city, then by their ca' and their scuola, and lastly by their mask. The Sonta call themselves citizens before anything else, and they measure a city by whether its offices are open, its courts honest, and its watch awake.

They count themselves heirs of Presium's seafaring and of Eldar law, and they reject Presium's rule by a closed circle of families. Many Sonta speak of the old oligarchy with the same distaste they hold for the faceless emperor.

## Adaptation & Tensions

The old families of Presium never vanished. Some of their descendants still hold great wealth, and in several cities they press for a smaller council and longer terms, claiming that seasoned hands govern best. Their rivals call this an attempt to close the council and bring back the oligarchy, and the argument flares whenever a Consegio votes on who may sit in it.

Masks invite abuse. Thieves, smugglers, and conspirators find them as useful as honest citizens do, and every few years a scandal breaks when an officeholder is found to have acted masked. The Lords of the Night argue for more power to unmask, and the Consegio guards the right of citizens to keep their faces hidden.

The Sonta share Korthak's eastern edge with its peoples and trade with them daily, though the rivalries of the city-state wars have not wholly faded. They watch the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Empire of Nox]] with wary respect. Nox's council governs, but the same Chaos Lord has stood above it for 170 years, and that sits uneasily with every Sonta instinct about power. The mines of the northern peaks bring the Sonta into regular contact with the Valekai, whose passes they rely on and whose ways they understand poorly.

## Distinctive Traits

- **Masks in public**, and a bare face for oaths, contracts, the courts, the home, and the dead
- **Barefaced officials**, who may not mask in public while they hold office
- **Masked voting** in the Consegio
- **The *tabaro***, a long wool cloak worn by men and women alike
- **Family masks** hung along the wall of an old house or scuola hall
