## Overview
Mechanus is where perfectly regimented order reigns. It holds equal measures of light and dark and equal proportions of heat and cold, and it is as predictable as the drip of a water clock and as obvious as a tree standing in a field. All law on Mechanus is reflected in a single infinite realm of clockwork gears, interlocked and turning each according to its own measure. The cogs appear to be engaged in a calculation so vast that no deity knows its purpose, beyond the fact that it is somehow a function of law.

The plane seems as straightforward as anything among the Outer Planes at a glance, and subtleties lurk beneath the surface. Every kind of law is found here, from simple maxims to devilishly twisted rules of decorum. For the most part Mechanus holds no passion, no illusion, and no pain. Perfection follows once all consciousness has been subsumed entirely into the whole.

Mechanus is a single infinite plane with no defined layers. Gargantuan interlocking wheels fill the void like the internal cogs of an ornately carved clock, some connecting at right angles and others along a single plane. The wheels, called cogs, are composed of stone, earth, and metal ores, as though a deity had carved them out of the mantle of the material world.

Many cogs measure more than a thousand miles across and turn so slowly that their rotation cannot be perceived. Turn they do nonetheless, as the much smaller island-sized cogs interlocking with them demonstrate. Those smaller cogs seem to spin at an almost dizzying pace, and yet an inhabitant standing on one feels nothing of the motion. The plane's gravity holds fast to each surface, so nobody is thrown off a cog's edge unless they stand within a few paces of the rim.

Sight is unaffected. A white, all-encompassing light springs from the void itself for twelve hours and gives way to twelve hours of darkness. Hearing is likewise unaffected, though the bass rumbling of cog turning against cog is sometimes audible near the edge of a face.

## Planar Links
Free-standing portals from elsewhere usually connect at the center of any given cog. Some lead to other Outer Planes, to Inner Planes, or to the Astral or Ethereal Planes. Some lead only to other cog faces.

Portals here appear as light green coglike circles, slowly turning. A green portal appears regularly once per revolution of the cog it sits on, though some appear according to the interaction of several cogs at once, and which cogs those are is not always apparent. Once open, a portal generally remains so for at least an hour and sometimes for a full day.

## Inhabitants
The inhabitants of Mechanus make their homes on the cogs turning in the void. Gear faces are normally free of vegetation and native wildlife, though colonists from other planes, including the material world, the Nine Hells, and Celestia, have transformed many faces into elaborate hedge mazes, parks, or nature reserves.

The most prevalent creatures here are not living creatures at all. They are constructs, known collectively as inevitables, and they exist to enforce the natural laws of the universe. Three types are widely recognized: kolyaruts, who hunt fugitives; maruts, who oppose those who cheat death; and zelekhuts, who enforce contracts. Each type relentlessly finds and punishes those who have committed the transgression it exists to answer.

## Petitioners
The petitioners of Mechanus often adopt a stylized version of their mortal bodies. Whatever their outward differences, all of them share a frightening honesty and a submersion of individuality. They are notoriously literal, and some take no instruction at all for fear of misinterpreting the speaker.

## Travel and Conflict
Moving about Mechanus is much like moving about the material world. Walking between turning cogs daunts the uninitiated and proves manageable with a little care. A serious misstep drops a traveler into the teeth of the gears, where the grinding is lethal and climbing back out is difficult work. Those able to fly or teleport cross the void between cogs without troubling themselves over any of this.

## Notable Locations

**Naumannus:** Inevitables are designed, constructed, and assigned tasks upholding various laws. Who created the first one is unknown, and the inevitables themselves claim ignorance of their own origin. Once created, the constructs soon learned the trick of replicating themselves. Naumannus is one such factory, occupying a small cog. It is a place devoid of ordinary life and crawling with inevitables at all hours. Tall smokestacks cover both sides of the cog and belch sooty clouds without pause from the fires raging in its forges and furnaces.

Inside, chamber after chamber is given over to crystal molds of dizzying variety. Special constructs cool, shape, and temper liquid metal in those molds. Finished pieces are assembled and animated in arcane baths of oil-like consistency, and after a brief shakedown the newly built inevitable is sent onto the Great Wheel with its first task. Tasks are magically encoded on the construct in glowing runes handed down by the Hub of Elders, and each factory keeps its own hub. The elders are several hundred special inevitables who spend their time peering through the cosmos for broken laws requiring amends. Inevitables sometimes bow to other noted authorities of law among the Outer Planes and do their bidding for a short period.

**Delon-Estin Oti:** A perfectly symmetrical town occupying only a small part of a far larger cog. It has twenty bordering walls with one gate on each side, and farmers till plots of fruits and vegetables and tend animals just inside those gates. Seen from above the town resembles a spiderweb, with concentric roads radiating from a hub that is a great open space of grass ringed by a single circle of trees at the very center.

The town is peaceful and tranquil, so thoroughly ordered and habitual that its citizens anticipate the course of time and the flow of conversation, and interrupt visitors who do not know what the future holds. Those seeking perfect equality and freedom from the lower and higher passions alike make their homes here. The population is drawn from every race on the Great Wheel, all equal, with no ruler over them. Many petitioners live within and around the town, happy to work the agricultural plots. Delon-Estin Oti has a reputation as a place where seers are born and where fortune-tellers gravitate, so visitors hoping to learn their fate are common.

**Fortress of Disciplined Enlightenment:** A structure sitting on its own cog, its spires and towers rising high into the void. The fortress is two miles in diameter and some of its spires reach twice that height. Inevitables enforcing the law against trespass patrol the parapets, watching for fiendish infiltrators and colonizing formians.

A group of mortals called the Fraternity of Order are masters of the fortress. Its members believe that if they can tease forth every law of the cosmos they will hold the power of deities, and to that end they built their stronghold on the plane of ultimate law. A horde of clerks, functionaries, legal aides, translators, mathematicians, philosophers, and bureaucrats staff the place, most of them members of the Fraternity, though visitors are sometimes granted access to study in the vaunted libraries. Those libraries extend through hundreds of rooms and hold legal volumes gathered from all over the Great Wheel.
