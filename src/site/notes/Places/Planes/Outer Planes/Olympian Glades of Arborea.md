## Overview
The Olympian Glades of Arborea are a crazy quilt of climates and environments, all of them thriving. Great woods of towering maple, birch, and oak strain skyward, leaving a forest floor relatively free of undergrowth and brush, and the ground beneath the canopy rolls in velvet moss and ferns. The forest sometimes retreats before open glades of wildflowers, fields of swaying wheat and barley, and neat rows of fruit trees that no hand tends. Here are trees that have never seen a woodsman's axe, fields rich with grain, and orchards heavy with fruit.

The air seems charged with anticipation and excitement. Sudden squalls brew out of nowhere and beat the tree-lined paths with heavy winds, then pass within minutes and leave behind warm sunny arcs of light filtering through the canopy. Music always seems to be playing somewhere in the distance. Sometimes the [[Species and Cultures/Races/Mannee\|Mannee]] and the [[Fey\|Fey]] are playing it. Just as often the faint tune is the wind curling through the boles of the great trees.

Flowers bloom and trees bear fruit at the same moment on Arborea. Uplands lie under snow, and even that snow shines beneath a crystal-blue sky. The plane is almost overwhelming in its beauty, and the land holds wilderness and loveliness together in one package.

Only the top layer carries the great forest the plane's name implies. The second layer is an endless ocean and the third a borderless desert of white dust. All three share mercurial weather, sudden attacks, and strong passions. Arborea is a plane of joy and sorrow.

## Planar Links
Arborea borders the Beastlands and Ysgard. Natural portals between them are common, as are shifting borders that move a traveler from one plane to its neighbor without any action on their part.

## Inhabitants
Celestials are common here, the [[Species and Cultures/Races/Mannee\|Mannee]] above all, who are powerful protectors of the plane.

Many azatas make their homes in Arborea, thriving on the emotions that ripple through it.

The plane is also home to many celestial or anarchic versions of creatures found in the material world. Celestial creatures generally leave travelers alone unless crossed, at which point they fight with relish. Anarchic creatures are as unreliable and unpredictable as their name suggests.

## Petitioners
Arborea has two common kinds of petitioner. The first are the spirits of the [[Species and Cultures/Races/Mannee\|Mannee]] dead, whose souls migrated here to their final reward. Some infuse the plane itself. Others are reformed into celestial or anarchic creatures, and these act as scouts and wardens for the plane's communities, serving the Mannee Court in its magic glades and great castles.

Other petitioners are found here besides the mannee souls. Called Bacchae, they are wild mobs of drunken revelers found reclining in glades or running through the forest in raving, wine-sotted celebration.

The Bacchae are satyrlike, caught in mid-transformation between man and beast. They are spirits of good and chaos in equal measure, living for the moment and making it the best moment possible. The greatest danger they pose is the pull of the celebration itself. A traveler who comes within earshot of a large enough mob finds it very hard to walk away, and those drawn in neither eat nor drink anything that sustains them, however much they seem to feast. The revel holds until the enticed collapses from want or until someone carries them well clear of the mob, and nobody under its sway leaves willingly. The Bacchae themselves are generally nonviolent and flee if attacked, or more likely offer bread and mead to their assailants. In life they were gourmands, gluttons, well-meaning drunkards, and others who relished the act of living.

## Travel and Conflict
Arborea imposes no penalty on movement beyond what a traveler from the material world would expect. On Arvandor, paths snake through the undergrowth, and clear glades are as common as patches of briar and tangle. Creatures able to climb or brachiate cross the forested sections without touching the ground at all. Aquallor, the second layer, is entirely aquatic, so anyone crossing it swims.

The plane itself neither aids nor hinders a fight. Cover and concealment are plentiful in the forests of Arvandor, while fighting in Aquallor is fighting underwater, with everything that implies.

## Layers

### Arvandor
Most travelers know Arvandor as the realm of the Mannee Court, the mythical Seldarine.

It is a place of great open spaces beneath the trees. Within those canopied clearings lie the settlements of the Chosen of Arvandor, engaged in an idealized mannee life: hunts and challenges by day, feasts and tales around the fire by evening.

The cycle of day and night matches the material world, with a golden sun beaming above the treetops and a white moon nestled in a milky river of stars at night. The nights are warm. Even beneath the trees, great fireflies form their own elastic constellations among the huge oaks and lindens.

**The Seldarine:** The masters of Arvandor rule with an artistic hand. Their halls may be natural, huge cathedrals grown from living trees, or wondrous palaces of crystal and white marble with golden towers, as befits the deity of such a noble race. Corellon Larethian's tower is the latter kind. Festooned with colorful banners, it dominates the surrounding countryside, and only mannee and Corellon's allies pass its doors to share in the song and the feast.

### Aquallor
The second layer is an eternal ocean, much like the Elemental Plane of Water, and vortices connect the two. Unlike that Inner Plane, Aquallor has both a surface and a bottom, and its lower reaches are as black as night.

What it lacks is islands. Great storms stalk the surface and swamp any boat that dares the heavy seas. Below the surface the water is absolute, and anything that cannot breathe it must bring its own means of doing so.

Like the first layer, Aquallor is subject to quicksilver changes in weather, which here take the form of great underwater currents that drag a traveler miles off course.

Sea life fills the layer, most of it celestial or anarchic in nature. Explorers who swim deep enough find larger beasts and aberrations.

Aquallor is the ending point of the River Oceanus, which weaves through many of the upper planes. Maelstroms in its depths may lead back to the river's headwaters, which would make the river a snake swallowing its own tail, endless in nature. Those seeking to leave Aquallor can be swept away by one of those maelstroms, at the risk of meeting less friendly creatures wherever they emerge.

### Mithardir
A desert of fine, white, chalky grit extending forever. The layer was once a great forest and home to creatures that campfire stories describe alternately as giants or titanlike deities. Why they no longer live here is unknown. Mithardir is empty now.

Persistent explorers still find the great towers and tombs of that lost race, their minarets breaking through the grit like fingers straining out of a premature grave. Brave travelers seek the layer out hoping to pry loose the secrets of the lost giants. More often than not, the incessant winds grind the bones of such explorers into powder.

As elsewhere on Arborea, the weather is sudden and dramatic. Lightning storms lash across the desert regularly, driving huge duststorms ahead of them, and a traveler can be buried by a single sudden assault.
