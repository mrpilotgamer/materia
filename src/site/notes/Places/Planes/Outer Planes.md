[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Peaceable Kingdoms of Arcadia\|Peaceable Kingdoms of Arcadia]] - Orderly orchards and geometric cities, so set on harmony that the plane has already lost one of its layers to Mechanus.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Seven Mounting Heavens of Celestia\|Seven Mounting Heavens of Celestia]] - One holy mountain climbed layer by layer, each layer with its own trial, toward the Heavenly City at the top.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Twin Paradises of Bytopia\|Twin Paradises of Bytopia]] - Two layers facing each other across a mile of sky, tame farmland on one side and wilderness on the other.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Blessed Fields of Elysium\|Blessed Fields of Elysium]] - Four layers strung along the River Oceanus, where kindness is the only law and visitors are tempted to stay forever.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Wilderness of the Beastlands\|Wilderness of the Beastlands]] - Endless wild forest, stuck at afternoon, dusk and night on its three layers, where the animals come first.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Olympian Glades of Arborea\|Olympian Glades of Arborea]] - Wild woods, a shoreless sea and a white desert, home of the Mannee Court and of moods that turn as fast as the weather.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Heroic Domains of Ysgard\|Heroic Domains of Ysgard]] - Floating continents of earth where dead heroes fight every day and get up again every morning.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Ever-Changing Chaos of Limbo\|Ever-Changing Chaos of Limbo]] - A churning soup of the elements that takes whatever shape a strong enough mind gives it, home of the githzerai and the slaadi.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Windswept Depths of Pandemonium\|Windswept Depths of Pandemonium]] - Endless dark tunnels where the wind never stops screaming, and most who stay long enough go mad.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Infinite Layers of the Abyss\|Infinite Layers of the Abyss]] - Layer after layer of horror, 666 at least, home of the demons and their lords.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Tarterian Depths of Carceri\|Tarterian Depths of Carceri]] - The prison plane: chains of small worlds where traitors and exiles plot their way back.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Gray Waste of Hades\|Gray Waste of Hades]] - A colorless land where hope drains away, and the main battlefield of the Blood War.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Bleak Eternity of Gehenna\|Bleak Eternity of Gehenna]] - Volcanic mountains hanging in the void, all slopes and no flat ground, where the yugoloths scheme.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Nine Hells of Baator\|Nine Hells of Baator]] - Nine layers stepping down into the pit, where Noir and his archdevils rule by contract and rank.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Infernal Battlefield of Acheron\|Infernal Battlefield of Acheron]] - Iron cubes crashing together in the void, and armies that have forgotten why they fight.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Clockwork Nirvana of Mechanus\|Clockwork Nirvana of Mechanus]] - Endless turning gears, and the inevitables who enforce the laws of the universe.

[[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Concordant Domains of the Outlands\|Concordant Domains of the Outlands]] - The neutral hub of the planes, where magic fades near the central Spire and Sigil, the City of Doors, floats above it.
