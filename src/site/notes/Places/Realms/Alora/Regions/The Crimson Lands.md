# The Crimson Lands
*"Land of Dragons"*

## Overview

The Crimson Lands, also known as the Land of Dragons, spans two continents and is believed to be the birthplace of the first dragons in the multiverse. Dragons still rule in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Narathatath\|Narathatath]], where they commonly fly through the major cities. Beneath the ground lie the cave systems the [[Species and Cultures/Races/Trox\|Trox]] once called home.

The region holds a good portion of the world's major trade centers, and a heavily defended route known as the Crimson Circuit connects all of its major cities. [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Laurland\|Laurland]], an island nation, lies to the west, between the Crimson Lands and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Jotuun Nexus\|The Jotuun Nexus]].

Each continent belongs to one nation. Narathatath holds the western continent and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/The Rayhostian Theocracy\|The Rayhostian Theocracy]] the eastern, and a narrow strait separates them. Both were once a single dragon-ruled territory, until the Human Uprising of 4287 divided it. The two have kept the peace for the past 170 years. In that time they have established multiple trade treaties through the diplomatic efforts of Slyrsomyr the Bridgemaker, a young adult black dragon and Narathatath's chief diplomat to the Theocracy.

## Climate & Terrain

The two continents lie side by side in the northern half of the world, and rainfall and temperature vary widely across them. Narathatath reaches farther south, with its southernmost islands near the equator, while the northern coasts of the Theocracy face the polar seas.

Narathatath's continent is mostly mountainous and volcanic, and a narrow isthmus joins its northwestern lobe to the main landmass. Kisondarket stands on the western coast near that isthmus. The [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Drako\|Drako]] culture there is highland-based, and the dragon-ruled territory is a land of deep mines and natural sulfur springs.

The Rayhostian Theocracy's continent is the larger of the two and mostly forest and plains. A deep bay reaches far into it from the southeast, and Hearhand stands near the head of that bay. Its fertile agricultural land is suited to grain and livestock, with hardwood forests of oak and maple, quarries of limestone and marble, and deposits of clay.

## Natural Resources

Narathatath is rich in metal and stone, with deep mines yielding diamonds, rubies, and emeralds. Its iron ore comes from ancient Trox mines and is counted the highest quality in the world's trade, and its coal deposits supply fuel for forging and heating. Gold and silver veins run throughout the territory, and the sulfur springs supply both alchemists and armies.

The Theocracy's farmland produces grain, livestock, and dairy for export. Its forests supply construction timber, and its quarries provide the marble worked into sculpture and religious art. Iron ore is present as well, though of moderate quality.

Each nation's exports appear on the other's import lists. Narathatath imports agricultural products, wooden goods, fine textiles, and rare books, as well as exotic foods, since its dragons demand variety in what they eat. The Theocracy buys precious metals for temple ornamentation, along with metal tools and weapons.

## Unique Features

Narathatath is governed by a council of ten dragons, by tradition five chromatic and five metallic, each of a different kind. Solanneth the Wise, a gold dragon more than 3,000 years old, is the most respected of the ten. Dragon-guarded vaults in the territory offer storage and security for high-value goods.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Riavexect, God of Want\|Riavexect, God of Want]], called the Dragon of the Crimson Lands, is the patron of the region. Worship of the god was particularly strong here, where dragons are believed to have first emerged, and dragons carried the faith outward as they spread across [[Places/Materia\|Materia]]. [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Alfugel, God of Hatred\|Alfugel, God of Hatred]] is also widely worshiped in a region that celebrates draconic might and strength.

Many scholars speculate that the first [[Species and Cultures/Races/Drakmed\|Drakmed]] also emerged in the Crimson Lands, though the exact nature of their descent from dragons remains unclear.

## Challenges

No external power threatens the Crimson Lands, though bandits and wild monsters keep the Crimson Circuit heavily defended. Relations between Narathatath and the Theocracy have been tenuous, and while they have improved over the past century, the two nations remain wary of each other.

Within Narathatath, metallic and chromatic dragons share power on the council despite an ongoing rivalry between the two lineages.

## Current Inhabitants

Narathatath governs from its capital of Kisondarket, population 11,427, and the Theocracy from Hearhand, population 25,370. Drako culture is dominant in Narathatath, and [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Anor\|Anor]] culture in the Theocracy.

The Theocracy is ruled primarily by humans and has been home to the largest Trox population for the past few hundred years. The Trox emergence to the surface is recent in their long history. Their culture, [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Trow\|Trow]], came up with them from the caverns and has since been shaped by both Drako and Anor. It is most common along the coasts of the strait, where the Trox surfaced on both shores. Trox communities hold Alfugel in special reverence, and the god is said to favor the form of a Trox.
