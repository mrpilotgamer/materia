# The Wildlands
*"Land of Many Origins"*

## Overview

The Wildlands is a long continent in the south of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Alora\|Alora]], lying across the water from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Jotuun Nexus\|The Jotuun Nexus]]. It has the thinnest barrier between the material world and the outer planes of any region in the realm, so clerics find it easier to reach their gods here, and demons find it easier to cross into [[Places/Materia\|Materia]]. Demon sightings are not an everyday event, but they are not rare either.

Four nations divide the continent today. The [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Empire of Nox]] holds the west, the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Republic of Korthak\|Republic of Korthak]] the west-centre, and the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Cilomutesan Union\|Cilomutesan Union]] a narrow strip running from coast to coast. The [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Empire of Bornumia]] holds everything east of the Union, well over half the continent.

Before 5267 the map looked different. The Empire of Adriacium held the west, where Nox and western Korthak stand now, and the Oligarchy of Presium held the centre, where the Union and eastern Korthak stand now. Bornumia was already an empire of its own, and somewhat larger than it is today.

Adriacium's emperor was murdered in 5167, in circumstances never explained. In his place rose an unknown, faceless emperor who gave Adriacium a single law, that no one may infringe on the freedom of another, and enforced no other. For a century Adriacium was a near-lawless state, where people of any race could live freely beside monsters and outsiders of every kind, demons, devils, and undead among them.

In 5267 an army of demons and devils marched out of Adriacium and ravaged the continent. The two had joined in an unholy alliance that did not always hold together, and the army reached into Bornumia before it was stopped.

The party [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Retribution\|Retribution]] brought reinforcements from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/The Rayhostian Theocracy\|the Rayhostian Theocracy]] and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imnas\|Imnas]] to halt the army inside Bornumia, and it broke apart when they banished Rouge that same year. Adriacium and Presium did not survive the war. [[Vera\|Vera]] of Retribution raised the Empire of Nox from Adriacium's ruins, and the city-states that emerged across the rest of Adriacium and Presium fought over what remained until Korthak and the Union took shape.

After the war it was revealed that the faceless emperor had been [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Rouge, the Red Progenitor Demon\|Rouge]] himself, but many refused to believe it. In the 170 years since, the claim has passed into myth and legend, and most now believe the war was a demon incursion gone wrong.

## Climate & Terrain

The Wildlands lies in the southern half of the world, and it grows colder toward the south. The northern coast is mild, while the southern coast faces seas choked with ice, and beyond them a vast shelf of ice runs along the whole length of the continent.

Temperate deciduous forest runs along the entire northern coast. Taiga covers the southern half of the continent from end to end, and it is the largest biome by far. Temperate rainforest grows only in the north of Nox, around Lincugus, and a wetland covers the peninsula at Nox's southwestern tip.

A broad grassland plain crosses northern Korthak and reaches into the north of the Union. Smaller stretches of grassland lie in western and eastern Bornumia. The continent's only mountain range runs northwest to southeast, from the Union's north into western Bornumia, and its peaks carry glaciers. Patches of tundra lie at the southern end of the range and at the tip of Bornumia's southern peninsula.

Bays and inlets break the southern coast. A large bay reaches into Nox from the south, Korthak's southern shore is ragged with inlets, and a great bay the Eldar call the *Mare Clausum*, the Closed Sea, nearly cuts Bornumia's eastern lobe away from the rest of the empire.

## Natural Resources

Forests are the continent's greatest resource. Its northern woods yield hardwood and its taiga yields timber and fur, while Bornumia's forests hold hardwood valued across [[Places/Realms/Alora/Alora\|Alora]] and game that is plentiful but often dangerous.

Adriacium's old copper mines lie in Nox, along with quarries of marble and limestone and vineyards in the milder north. Korthak has rich silver mines, farmland on its central plain, livestock, and stone quarries. The Union has excellent natural harbors, rich fishing grounds, coastal forests for shipbuilding, and salt from evaporation ponds along its coasts.

The thin planar barrier leaves its mark on some resources. Bornumia mines demon-touched iron, ore strengthened by planar energy, in small and dangerous quantities, and rare medicinal plants grow only in its planar-touched soil.

## Unique Features

The barrier between the Wildlands and the outer planes is thin enough that extraplanar beings and effects cross more easily here than anywhere else in Alora, and even weaker demons can make a crossing that would be beyond them elsewhere. The same thinness brings the gods closer, and clerics speak of prayers answered more readily here.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Malbing, God of Consumption\|Malbing]] bears the epithet Beast of the Wildlands, and their worship is strongest here. They are the region's most honored god, though no nation claims them as its patron. Farmers honor them for the harvest, and their teaching that all things are consumed and returned carries weight in a land that remembers the war of 5267.

The ruins of Adriacium lie across the west, in the lands the faceless emperor ruled for a century. Nox has restored and expanded much of what the empire built, but ruins remain beyond its cities. The ruins of Presium lie in the centre, in the lands Korthak and the Union now divide.

The mountain range is home to the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Valekai\|Valekai]], who held to their heights through the war of 5267 and were only lightly touched by it, though the clans of the northernmost peaks abandoned them and never returned.

The ice shelf beyond the southern coast is uninhabited and unsafe to live on. Some have tried to cross it, and none who went past its edge have returned.

## Challenges

Cold is the continent's constant hardship. Winters are long across the taiga and the southern coast is bound in sea ice, so settlement thins toward the south and the population gathers along the milder northern coast.

Demon sightings are a regular hazard. Weaker demons cross the thin barrier often enough that most communities keep some defense against them.

The three western nations are still feeling their growing pains. Korthak and the Union were forged out of city-states that fought one another for power after the fall, and the borders and rivalries of that time have not wholly settled. Nox holds a great deal of soft power over its neighbors, and Vera is a known factor in every decision made around her.

## Current Inhabitants

The Empire of Nox governs the west from Lincugus, population 19,961. Korthak governs from Saventiaria, population 12,431, and the Cilomutesan Union from Diaquingis, population 12,428. The Empire of Bornumia governs the east from Conatagus, population 50,413, one of the most populous cities in Alora.

[[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Eldar\|Eldar]] culture once covered the whole continent. It lost its hold in the war of 5267 and has receded into Bornumia, the heartland where it was born. Four newer cultures have grown up in its place, each drawing on Eldar and each with its own values: the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Algar\|Algar]] in Nox, the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Barbois\|Barbois]] in northern Korthak, the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Casta\|Casta]] in southern Korthak, and the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Sonta\|Sonta]] in the Union. The Valekai keep to the mountains, as they have for as long as the archive records.

Bornumia has the highest density of [[Species and Cultures/Races/Daemedi\|Daemedi]] anywhere in Alora. [[Species and Cultures/Races/Human\|Humans]] and [[Species and Cultures/Races/Vullukk\|Vullukk]] are common across the continent, the Valekai are almost purely Vullukk, and other races are present in small numbers.
