# Republic of Korthak

## Overview

The Republic of Korthak holds the west-centre of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]], on land that was once divided between the Empire of Adriacium and the Oligarchy of Presium. It was born from the city-state wars that followed their fall in 5267, and it took its present form around 5347, when every power within its borders signed a founding treaty.

Korthak is a republic of two cultures. The [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Barbois\|Barbois]] hold the north and the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Casta\|Casta]] the south, and each governs its own provinces in its own way beneath a national government deliberately built so that no single person rules it. The two cultures quarrel often, but they sit between powerful neighbors and cannot afford to let their quarrels break the republic.

## Geography

Korthak stretches between the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Empire of Nox]] to the west and the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Cilomutesan Union\|Cilomutesan Union]] to the east, and it reaches both coasts of the continent. Its western half was once part of Adriacium, and its eastern half part of Presium.

The north is Barbois country. A broad grassland plain crosses it, the best farmland and grazing on the continent, and temperate forest runs along the northern coast, where the capital, Saventiaria, stands a short way inland.

The south is Casta country, and it is colder and rougher. Taiga covers it, cut by sheltered valleys and ending in a ragged coast of peninsulas and inlets, with a deep inlet reaching north from the southern sea. Most of the south's people live in the valleys and on the peninsulas. A few hardy families hold the far southern tip, where the taiga meets the frozen sea.

## Government

The founders of Korthak set out to make sure that no single person could ever rule it. Its national government is divided into three branches, each checking the others, and none has a single head of state.

The **Consulate** is the executive. It is led by two Consuls who serve together for a term of four years, and neither may act on matters of state without the other's agreement. By custom established at the founding and never broken, one Consul comes from the Barbois provinces and one from the Casta provinces. The Consuls command the national army, conduct relations with foreign powers, and carry out the laws.

The **Assembly** is the legislature, and it sits in two chambers. The Council of Provinces gives each province an equal number of seats, and the Chamber of Delegates divides its seats among the provinces by population. A law must pass both chambers, and together they elect the Consuls. Each province chooses its members in its own way: the Barbois through elections held by their communes and provincial assemblies, and the Casta through their counts and barons.

The **Tribunal** is the high court. Its seven judges are named by the Consuls and confirmed by the Council of Provinces, and they serve for life. The Tribunal settles disputes between provinces, rules on whether a law or an act of the Consuls breaks the founding treaty, and hears appeals from the provincial courts.

Below the national government, each province governs itself according to its culture. Barbois provinces are run by elected assemblies and communes, and Casta provinces by their counts and barons. The founding treaty guarantees both ways of life, and the Tribunal is charged with defending that guarantee.



## Law

Korthak's law rests on the Eldar legal codes, which both cultures inherited and which the founding treaty names as the common foundation of the republic. National law passed by the Assembly stands above provincial law, and provincial law governs everything the Assembly leaves alone.

The two cultures read the same codes very differently. Barbois courts treat every citizen as an equal before them, while Casta courts hear the cases of their lords and familie in the old way. The Casta duel is lawful in Casta provinces within limits the Assembly has set, and it is forbidden in Barbois provinces. Disputes that cross the line between the two are among the most common cases before the Tribunal.

## Military

Korthak keeps a national army under the command of the Consuls, recruited from every province and garrisoned along the borders and the roads. By tradition guaranteed in the founding treaty, Casta counts and barons also keep their own armed retinues. The retinues answer to their lords in peacetime and join the national army when the Consuls call them to war.

Korthak keeps a small coastal fleet on the northern sea to guard its harbors and trade. The Casta coast of the south is too ice-bound for much naval use.

The balance between the national army and the lords' retinues is a lasting source of tension. Barbois politicians call the retinues private armies, and Casta lords call them the guarantee that no Consul can ever turn the army against them.

## Economy

Korthak is among the most productive nations in the Wildlands. The northern plain grows grain and grazes horses, cattle, and sheep, and the republic feeds itself with a surplus to sell. Its forests supply timber and its quarries stone, and the southern taiga yields furs and game.

Silver is Korthak's greatest treasure, and most of it lies in the Casta south. The silver mines have made the southern counts and the banking familie of the Casta rich. The northern Barbois hold the grain, the capital, and the larger population. That division of wealth underlies much of the politics between the two cultures.

Korthak imports iron and steel, tools and machinery, books, luxury goods, and precision instruments.

## Peoples and Culture

Korthak is the only nation in the Wildlands where two cultures share power. The Barbois dominate the north and the Casta the south. The [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Algar\|Algar]] of Nox reach across the western border into the northwest, and the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Sonta\|Sonta]] of the Union into the east.

No race makes up a majority in either culture. [[Species and Cultures/Races/Daemedi\|Daemedi]] are the most common, followed closely by [[Species and Cultures/Races/Human\|Humans]] and [[Species and Cultures/Races/Vullukk\|Vullukk]], and members of every other race live among them.

## Relations

Korthak sits between the Empire of Nox and the Cilomutesan Union, and it watches both. Nox holds a great deal of soft power over its neighbors, and it reaches into Korthak in ways both cultures feel. Casta counts have sought Vera's favor, and Barbois politicians warn against it, so the Consuls tread carefully in every dealing with Nox.

The Union is Korthak's partner in trade and its old rival from the city-state wars. The two republics divided the lands of Presium between them, and a few old claims along their border are still argued over. Farther east, the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Empire of Bornumia]] is distant, and its senators' talk of the western cultures as lost Eldar branches is received coldly in Saventiaria.

## History

Before 5267 the land that is now Korthak was split between the Empire of Adriacium in the west and the Oligarchy of Presium in the east. For a century before the fall, its western half lived under Adriacium's faceless emperor.

Both Adriacium and Presium fell in the [[History/Timeline\|war of 5267]]. Their people were killed or scattered, and the survivors rebuilt from the ruins. Captains and warlords seized the cities, and for decades the city-states fought one another for power. In the north, the cities rose against their captains and declared their people free and equal citizens, and those risings gave birth to the Barbois. In the south the captains held on, turned their conquests into counties, and became the Casta lords.

The wars wore both sides down. Around 5347, every power within Korthak's present borders met at Saventiaria and signed the Treaty of Saventiaria, founding the republic. The treaty guaranteed each culture its own way of governing, divided the national government into three branches, and made the Eldar codes the common law of all, and Korthak has held together under it ever since.

The republic's politics have never been quiet. The Barbois and the Casta clash over the army, the silver, the duel, and the reach of the national government, and every election to the Assembly is fought along that line. Neither side has ever pushed the other far enough to break the treaty, since both know how little stands between Korthak and its neighbors.

## Capital

Saventiaria, population 12,431, stands inland near the northern coast. It is the seat of the Consulate, the Assembly, and the Tribunal, and the place where the founding treaty was signed. It is a Barbois city in its architecture and customs, and the Casta provinces keep grand houses there for their delegates.
