# Oligarchy of Presium

<!-- GAP: Presium's capital, its exact founding date, and the names of its great families are lost. -->

## Overview

The Oligarchy of Presium was a maritime power in the centre of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]], ruled by a council of great merchant families. It was young as Wildlands realms go, founded only shortly before the century of Adriacium's faceless emperor, and it lasted about a hundred years. In 5267 the army of demons and devils that marched out of Adriacium outmaneuvered Presium's armies and crushed them within weeks, and the oligarchy was wiped away.

The [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Cilomutesan Union\|Cilomutesan Union]] and the eastern half of the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Republic of Korthak\|Republic of Korthak]] now stand on its lands. Its capital lies in ruins, and its name has been lost.

## Geography

Presium held the centre of the continent, the lands that are now the Union and eastern Korthak. Its cities stood along the northern coast and the rivers that ran down to it, in country of temperate forest and grassland. The northern end of the continent's mountain range rose on its eastern side, and its southern lands were taiga running down to the ice.

Its capital stood somewhere in those lands, and it lies in ruins today under a name no one now living remembers.

## Government

Presium was governed by a council of its great merchant families. Seats on the council belonged to the families themselves and passed down through their lines, and the families kept the council closed to newcomers, however rich. Between them they held the oligarchy's offices, its fleets, and most of its wealth.

The oligarchy was harder and narrower than the merchant republics that followed it. Presium was a state run for its great families, and the families ran it for themselves. The Sonta who descend from its people have made the rejection of that rule part of who they are.

## Military

Presium's strength was its fleet, which guarded its harbors and trade along the northern sea. Its armies were smaller, raised and paid by the great families, and they had never faced an enemy like the one that came out of Adriacium in 5267. The army of demons and devils outmaneuvered them and crushed them within weeks.

## Economy

Presium lived by the sea. Its harbor cities built ships, fished the northern waters, made salt along the coast, and carried goods between the realms of the Wildlands. It kept good trade relations with Adriacium both before and during the Faceless Century, and with the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Empire of Bornumia]] to the east.

## Peoples and Culture

Presium was an Eldar realm, and its people kept Eldar law and spoke the Eldaran of the central coast. Its coastal cities had long been a thing apart from the rest of the Eldar world, oriented toward the sea and toward trade. Masks were already part of Presian festivals before the oligarchy fell.

The [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Sonta\|Sonta]] descend from Presium's people, and they count themselves heirs of its seafaring and of Eldar law. They reject its rule by a closed circle of families, and they speak of the old oligarchy with nearly the same distaste they hold for the faceless emperor. Some descendants of the old families still hold great wealth in the Sonta cities.

## History

Presium was founded shortly before 5167, when the great merchant families of the central coast bound their cities together into a single oligarchy. Within a few years its western neighbor, Adriacium, fell under the faceless emperor. Presium went on trading with Adriacium through the century that followed, and its families profited from a neighbor whose commerce no law could bind.

In 5267 an army of demons and devils marched out of Adriacium. Presium's armies were outmaneuvered and crushed within weeks, and the oligarchy was wiped away. Its capital was ruined and its great families killed or scattered.

In the years after the war, the surviving cities fought one another for power. In the east, the harbor cities turned away from rule by a few great families, built merchant republics, and in 5330 bound themselves together as the Cilomutesan Union. The western lands of the old oligarchy became part of the Republic of Korthak.

## The Ruins Today

The ruins of Presium's capital lie in the lands the Union and Korthak now divide, and both republics have claimed the ground at one time or another. The few old claims still argued along their border include the ruins themselves. Treasure hunters search them for the vaults of the great families, which held the oligarchy's wealth and were never recovered.

The field where Presium's armies were crushed lies somewhere along the path the army took eastward, and it has an evil reputation. Abandoned harbor towns along the coast, emptied in 5267 and never resettled, are said to shelter demons left behind after the war. The Lords of the Night of the nearest Sonta cities keep watch on them.
