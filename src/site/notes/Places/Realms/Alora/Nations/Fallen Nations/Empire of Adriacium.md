# Empire of Adriacium



## Overview

The Empire of Adriacium was the great [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Eldar\|Eldar]] power of the western [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]]. It ruled from Lincugus, traded with its neighbors, and grew into one of the wealthiest realms of the Eldar world. In 5167 its emperor was murdered, and for a century afterward Adriacium lived under a faceless emperor who gave it a single law. In 5267 the faceless emperor's army marched out to ravage the continent, and the empire did not survive the war that followed.

Much of what was known about Adriacium was lost with it. Its official records perished in the ruins, and the archivists piece its history together from the accounts of survivors, the records of its neighbors, and the stones it left behind. The [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Empire of Nox]] now stands on its western lands.

## Geography

Adriacium held the west of the continent, the lands that are now the Empire of Nox and the western half of the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Republic of Korthak\|Republic of Korthak]]. Its heartland was the temperate rainforest and forest of the north, around its capital at Lincugus on the western coast. Its southern lands were taiga, and a great bay, the Adriac Gulf, reached into them from the southern sea.

The empire was rich in resources. Copper mines, marble and limestone quarries, vineyards, and fertile river valleys made it rich, and its roads and aqueducts were among the finest the Eldar ever built.

## Government

Adriacium was ruled by an emperor who held sole power. The details of how the empire was governed beneath the throne were lost with its records, and the archivists know little of its dynasties or its institutions.

In 5167 the last emperor was murdered in circumstances that were never explained, and his name is no longer remembered. A faceless emperor took the throne in his place, and no one in Adriacium ever learned who he was. He gave the empire a single law, that no one may infringe on the freedom of another, and he enforced no other. Whatever offices and courts survived from the old empire either faded away or carried on with no authority behind them.

After 5267 it was revealed that the faceless emperor had been [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Rouge, the Red Progenitor Demon\|Rouge]] himself. Many refused to believe it, and most now count the claim as legend.

## Military

Little is recorded of Adriacium's armies before 5167. In the Faceless Century, with no law but one, the empire kept no army that answered to the state. Its people defended themselves as best they could, or relied on whoever was strong enough to protect them.

The army that marched out of Adriacium in 5267 was no army of the empire. It was a horde of demons and devils, bound in an unholy alliance that did not always hold together, and it ravaged the continent until it was broken.

## Economy

Before 5167 Adriacium was wealthy. It mined copper, quarried marble and limestone, made wine, and farmed its river valleys, and it kept good trade relations with both the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Oligarchy of Presium\|Oligarchy of Presium]] and the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Empire of Bornumia]].

During the Faceless Century trade went on for those strong enough or clever enough to keep it going. With no law of contract, no courts to enforce a debt, and no state to guard a road, commerce became a matter of personal power and personal risk, and for every trader who grew rich, many more lost everything.

## Peoples and Culture

Adriacium was an Eldar realm, and its people kept Eldar law, language, and custom for most of its history. In the Faceless Century, people of every race lived there beside monsters and outsiders of every kind, demons, devils, and undead among them.

Those who lived through that century remembered it in two ways at once. For some it was a time of freedom and choice, when no one could tell them how to live. For others it was a time of terror. Which it was depended on who a person was, where they were born, and whether someone or something more powerful decided to ruin them.

The [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Algar\|Algar]] of Nox descend from Adriacium's survivors. They honor the empire's long history before 5167 and remember the century that followed as the Faceless Century, a warning of what freedom becomes without goodness and order.

## History

Adriacium was founded after the Empire of Bornumia, though the date has been lost with its records. It grew into one of the great powers of the Wildlands and kept peace and trade with its neighbors for most of its history.

In 5167 the emperor was murdered, and the faceless emperor took the throne. For a century Adriacium lived under his single law. Its neighbors watched with alarm, and Bornumia closed its western border as far as it could.

In 5267 an army of demons and devils marched out of Adriacium and ravaged the continent. Within months the western lands fell to ruin, their people killed or scattered. The party [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Retribution\|Retribution]] fought the army in Adriacium, brought reinforcements from across the ocean, and banished Rouge, and the army broke apart. Adriacium did not survive. [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Members/Vera\|Vera]] of Retribution raised the Empire of Nox from its ruins that same year, and the city-states that rose in its eastern lands became part of the Republic of Korthak.

## The Ruins Today

Nox has rebuilt many of Adriacium's cities, Lincugus first among them, but ruins remain across the west. Towns abandoned in the Faceless Century stand empty in the forests, and old roads lead to places no one has lived in for generations.

Some ruins are dangerous. Demons left behind after 5267 hide in them, and some of the outsiders who lived freely in the Faceless Century left their mark on the places they inhabited. Old graveyards and tombs that were left unwatched for a century draw the attention of the Phylakes, who keep watch on any site where the dead may not rest. Somewhere in the ruins lie the lost archives of the old empire, and the treasuries of families who fled or died in the war.

The palace where the faceless emperor held court stood in Lincugus. Nox did not rebuild it. What remains of it is sealed and guarded by the Phylakes, and the council has never explained to the public what lies inside.
