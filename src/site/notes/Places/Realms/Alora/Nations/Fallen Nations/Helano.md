# Helano



## Overview

Helano was a khanate of the central desert of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Jotuun Nexus\|The Jotuun Nexus]], and for nearly three centuries it was the greatest power on the continent. It was the heartland of [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Jotuun\|Jotuun]] culture and the homeland of the [[Species and Cultures/Races/Yol-Mar\|Yol-Mar]], and its people held the desert to be the ancient land of the giants. Helano was ruled by the Khan, one of the strongest Yol-Mar in the land, and its rule was militaristic throughout. It conquered [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Hovden\|Hovden]] and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Kingdom of Stamvia\|Stamvia]] and pressed hard against the east. In 5267 plague brought it down, and its lands have stood abandoned since.

## Geography

Helano held the heart of the continent. Most of its territory was hot desert, marked by the salt flats of ancient inland seas and by formations of natural glass. Toward its borders the desert gave way to savanna. At its height, the khanate's rule reached far beyond its own lands, north into the forests of Hovden and west to the grasslands and harbors of Stamvia.

## Government

The Khan ruled Helano, and the title belonged to Helano alone. It was held by one of the strongest Yol-Mar in the land, and in keeping with Jotuun custom, it went to the strongest, and birth gave no claim to it. Beneath the Khan, the khanate was governed as a military state, and its clans answered to the Khan as soldiers answer to a commander.

Conquered lands were ruled at a distance. Hovden kept its own kings, but the Khan approved each one, and Hovdeners still argue over whether he passed over the rightful heirs for more pliant cousins. Stamvia kept its republican government for its own internal affairs while answering to Helano in everything else.

## Military

Helano was built for war. Its armies were drawn from the desert clans, and its Yol-Mar warriors, whose weapons burn as they strike, were feared across the Nexus. What Helano lacked, it took. It seized Stamvia's fleet and shipbuilders to gain a navy, and took great numbers of Stamvia's horses, which the western herds have never recovered.

## Economy

The desert alone could not sustain the khanate, and Helano lived on what it drew from its conquests. Hovden sent timber and forest goods south to the desert heartland. Stamvia gave up its stonemasons, its ships, and its horses. The desert itself yielded salt, glass, and the goods of its scattered oases. Helano's dependence on its vassals was its greatest weakness, and it left the heartland with nothing to fall back on once the supplies stopped.

## Peoples and Culture

Helano was the heartland of [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Jotuun\|Jotuun]] culture, the desert made into a state. Its Jotuun held the harshest standards of their people and claimed the closest bond to the fire giants of legend. The [[Species and Cultures/Races/Yol-Mar\|Yol-Mar]] were Helano's own people, and the khanate's claim to be the land of the giants rested on them. Their fire was real for anyone to see, though no one has ever proven their descent from the giants.

## History

Helano's founding is not recorded. By the sixth millennium it had grown into the dominant power of the Nexus.

In 5017 Helano subjugated Hovden, then an independent kingdom, and ruled it through vassal kings. In 5067 it subjugated Stamvia, seeking the republic's stonemasons and its navy.

Helano's ambitions then turned east, and its court called the eastern coast "Khania," the Khan's land-to-be, and its envoys used the name to mock the quarrelling city-states there. In 5117 those cities united as the republic of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Khania\|Khania]] and took the name in defiance. With [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Laurland\|Laurland]]'s fleet holding the coast, Helano never reached the Dali shore.

Helano fell in 5267. A widespread plague swept the khanate and brought famine with it, and the state collapsed from within. The disease reached Hovden too, and by the time anyone understood what was happening there was no Helano left to supply. Hovden and Stamvia regained their independence. The archivists record that Helano's fall came in the same year as the falls of Adriacium and Presium, and judge the timing a coincidence.

## The Ruins Today

Most of Helano's survivors, the greater part of them Yol-Mar, scattered across the Nexus. The largest numbers went north into Hovden and west into Stamvia, where Yol-Mar communities already stood, and in Stamvia they now make up roughly half the population. A few stubborn clans refused to leave and remain in the desert as independent peoples, keeping the old desert standards of the Jotuun.

The ruins of Helano now shelter those clans and bands of outlaws. Raiders and monsters have grown more common in the interior since the fall. The Great Desert Road keeps to claimed land along the desert's edges, and Hovden watches its southern border from a line of forts.
