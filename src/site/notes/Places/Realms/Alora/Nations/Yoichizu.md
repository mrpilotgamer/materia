# Yoichizu

<!-- GAP: The current shōgun and their clan are unnamed. -->

## Overview

Yoichizu is a shogunate holding the south and east of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Azure Expanse\|The Azure Expanse]], and the homeland of the [[Species and Cultures/Races/Araki\|Araki]]. Its warrior clans have been united under a single shōgun for about six hundred and thirty years, and its sea clans command one of the strongest fleets in the region. Its smiths are renowned, though most of the iron they work comes from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Pimestalia\|Pimestalia]], a dependence Yoichizu accepts for now and means to end. Beyond its trade with its neighbors, Yoichizu keeps to itself.

## Geography

Yoichizu holds the south-centre of the continent and the whole of its long eastern arm. It has the longest coastline of the four nations: the north shore of the eastern arm faces Morlas Bay, and the south and east face the open sea. Two bays cut into the southern coast, and Killow stands at the head of the deeper one.

Yoichizu has no mountains. Temperate deciduous forest covers the country around Killow, temperate rainforest fills most of the eastern arm, and the whole of the northeastern peninsula is wetland. That wetland is the old heartland of Aj'Snaga culture, where the first clans built their houses on stilts and grew rice in the flooded fields.

Yoichizu borders [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imestalia\|Imestalia]] to the west and Pimestalia to the north. Across Morlas Bay lies [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imnas\|Imnas]], which holds the easternmost island taken from Yoichizu four centuries ago.

## Government

Yoichizu is ruled by a *shōgun*, a single warlord above the lords of the clans. There is no emperor or other figurehead above the shōgun.

The title passes on the shōgun's death. The clans then hold a tournament, and the head of the clan that wins it becomes the new shōgun. Each clan sends its champions to compete in arms, archery, and command, and victory brings a clan the highest honor in the nation. The tournament is held during the dead shōgun's tsuya, so a new shōgun is chosen before the old one wakes, and until it ends the clan lords govern together.

A shōgun who dies returns a month later as the newest and lowest member of their own clan, with no claim to the title they once held.

Beneath the shōgun, each clan is governed by its *sōryō*, who rules the clan's lands, ships, and people. The sōryō owe the shōgun loyalty and service, but within their own lands they govern as they see fit.

## Law

The shōgun's law binds every clan, and the sōryō judge the people of their own clans.

*Shūen*, the true death, may be ordered in only two ways: by the shōgun alone, or by a unanimous vote of every clan lord besides the shōgun. The second path means that, in principle, the clan lords together could pass the sentence on a shōgun.

## Military

Each clan commands its own forces. The sea clans hold the fleets, and the land clans raise the armies from their bushi and retainers. The shōgun has the authority to summon all the clans at once, and when the shōgun calls, every clan's forces march or sail under a single command.

Yoichizu's fleets are its pride. They range the open sea and Morlas Bay, and the great shipyard at Killow builds the largest of their ships. The bay has been neutral waters since the war with Imnas four centuries ago, and Yoichizu accepts that settlement for now, though some sea clans test it once in a while.

Yoichizu regards the skyships of Imnas as a threat. It has no ships that fly, but its castles, harbors, and larger warships mount ballistae and other heavy weapons able to strike at targets in the air.

## Economy

Weapons and armor are Yoichizu's great export, forged by its renowned smiths and sold mostly to Imestalia. Rice from the wetlands, fish, salt, and timber from the forests feed the nation and supply its trade. Yoichizu deals only with the nations of the Azure Expanse and is otherwise isolated from the wider world.

Most of its iron comes from Pimestalia, since Yoichizu has little of its own. The Aj'Snaga dislike depending on others for the metal at the heart of their craft and for the money it brings, but they accept the trade for now, until a way to supply themselves can be found.

## Peoples and Culture

Yoichizu is the homeland of the Araki, who are the great majority of its people by a wide margin, and its culture is Aj'Snaga throughout. Its people live in clans under the crests of their lords, in stilt houses in the wetlands and timber houses in the forests, and in castle towns that grow up around the strongholds of the clans. The few members of other races who live among them are mourned with special weight when they die, since they will not return.

## Relations

Relations with Imnas remain tense four centuries after the war over Morlas Bay, and the loss of the island is still remembered. Yoichizu trades with Pimestalia for iron and resents needing to, and it sells its weapons to Imestalia, Pimestalia's rival. Beyond the Azure Expanse it has few dealings with anyone.

## History

Aj'Snaga culture began in the wetlands of the northeastern peninsula and spread along the coasts and forests from there. About six hundred and thirty years ago the clans were unified under the first shōgun, and Yoichizu has been a shogunate ever since. <!-- GAP: How the clans were unified, and who the first shōgun was. -->

About four hundred years ago, Yoichizu laid claim to the whole of Morlas Bay, meaning to keep its navy strong and the bay under its control. Imnas retaliated and took the easternmost island. The war ended in an agreement that the bay would be neutral waters, and the island stayed with Imnas.

## Capital

Killow, population 25,492, stands at the head of a deep bay on the southern coast. It is the greatest castle town in Yoichizu, built around a great castle whose walls look over the harbor. Below the castle lies the great shipyard, where the largest ships of Yoichizu's fleets are built and launched.

## Pronunciation

Yoichizu (yoh-ee-CHEE-zoo), Killow (KIL-oh), shōgun (SHOH-gun), sōryō (SOH-ryoh)
