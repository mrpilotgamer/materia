# Imnas

## Overview

Imnas is a mageocracy holding the northern half of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Azure Expanse\|The Azure Expanse]], and the homeland of the [[Species and Cultures/Races/Mannee\|Mannee]]. It is governed from Sireth, the only floating city in the world, by a council of mages whose members no one outside the council knows. Its people are [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Dulandi\|Dulandi]], the oldest culture still strong in the region by most accounts, and its university at Sireth draws students from across [[Places/Realms/Alora/Alora\|Alora]]. Imnas keeps largely to itself, trading with its neighbors but seldom involving itself in their affairs.

## Geography

Imnas holds the north of the continent: a broad northeastern lobe, a northern peninsula, a western arm reaching south toward the mountains, and a southern peninsula that pushes into Morlas Bay. Hills rise across the centre, and rivers run down to every coast. Temperate deciduous forest covers the north and west, temperate rainforest fills the centre and east, and the southern peninsula is wetland.

Imnas borders [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imestalia\|Imestalia]] and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Pimestalia\|Pimestalia]] to the south, where its western arm meets the strip of tropical forest along the northern foot of the mountains. Its eastern and southern shores face Morlas Bay and the open sea, and the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|Crimson Lands]] lie across a narrow strait to the northwest. Imnas also holds the small islands off its northern and eastern coasts, the farthest of them the easternmost island, taken from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Yoichizu\|Yoichizu]] about four hundred years ago.

## Government

Imnas is governed by a council of twenty-three mages, one for each of the twenty-three known spheres of magic. The members are kept secret to protect them from attack and sabotage. Every member knows the others, but no one outside the council knows who sits on it, and any mage in Sireth might be one of them.

The Dulandi call it the *Cyngor Cudd*, the Hidden Council. The council speaks to the nation through sealed decrees and to foreign powers through envoys, who carry its word without revealing whose word it is.

When a seat falls empty, the council appoints whoever it knows to be the most capable mage in that sphere. An appointment needs the agreement of more than four in five members, at least nineteen of the twenty-three. The chosen mage may be a famous scholar of the university, a quiet pennaeth in the forests, or someone no one outside their household has heard of, since the council's knowledge reaches further than its secrecy suggests.

The households of the forests govern themselves at the local level. Each aelwyd runs its own affairs under its pennaeth, and villages settle their own matters, but the council's decisions bind the whole nation and are obeyed as such.

## Law

The council makes national law and judges the gravest cases, and magic plays a part in nearly every judgment it can. Divination, mind magic, and other workings are used to uncover the truth wherever possible, and a court without a mage is thought hardly a court at all. Below the council, the households and villages settle their own disputes in the Dulandi way.

## Military

Battle mages are the heart of the Imnas military, supported by soldiers drawn from the households. Few forces in Alora can match its mages, and in 5267 it was strong enough to send reinforcements across the ocean at [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Retribution\|Retribution]]'s urging. Those reinforcements helped halt the army of demons and devils inside the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Empire of Bornumia]].

Imnas also keeps a navy. Some of its ships are more than seaworthy: built with levitation stone and bound with enchantments, they can lift off the water and take to the sky. These skyships are rare and costly, and they are the pride of the fleet.

## Economy

Magic items are the great export of Imnas, made by its mages and sold across Alora. Levitation stone, found nowhere else in the world, is controlled by the state, and the schools are granted access to it only for research. The council treats it as a military resource, the foundation of the skyships and of Imnas's command of the air, and none of it leaves the nation's borders.

The university at Sireth and the smaller schools across the nation draw students from every corner of Alora, some coming to learn and others sent by their families or their rulers. Imnas also produces timber, medicinal herbs, and the work of its windmills. It imports metals and the raw materials of its enchanters, along with textiles and books.

## Peoples and Culture

Imnas is the homeland of the Mannee, who are the great majority of its people, and its culture is Dulandi throughout. The people of the forests live in households built into the trees and hills, while Sireth's mages live aloft in a modern city of stone and glass. The forest households count the mages of Sireth as their own, but find them distant.

The easternmost island is the exception. Its people are [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Aj'Snaga\|Aj'Snaga]] in culture and keep the customs of Yoichizu, but after four centuries under Imnas they are mostly indifferent to who rules them.

## Relations

Imnas keeps largely to itself beyond the business of trade. Its relations with Yoichizu remain tense four centuries after the war over Morlas Bay, and the Aj'Snaga have not forgotten the island. With Imestalia and Pimestalia it trades and teaches, and many of their mages train at Sireth. In 5267 its mages fought beside those of the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/The Rayhostian Theocracy\|Rayhostian Theocracy]], but the two have kept to trade since.

## History

The Dulandi have lived in the forests of Imnas longer than any record they keep, and the founding of the nation is lost to memory. <!-- NOTE: The founding of Imnas as a nation, as distinct from the Dulandi culture, has no date. Worth deciding whether it matters. -->

Sireth was raised into the sky more than five hundred years ago. Who raised it, and how long the work took, is known only to the council. The rest of Imnas knows only that one generation lived without a floating city and the next lived beneath it.

About four hundred years ago, Yoichizu laid claim to the whole of Morlas Bay, meaning to keep its navy strong and its waters closed. Imnas retaliated and took the easternmost island from Yoichizu. The war ended in an agreement that Morlas Bay would be neutral waters, open to all three nations that border it, and the island stayed with Imnas.

In 5267 the army of demons and devils marched out of Adriacium. Retribution pressed its danger on Imnas, and the council sent battle mages across the ocean to the Wildlands, where they helped halt the army inside Bornumia.

## Capital

Sireth, population 4,732, hangs more than 2,000 feet above the mouth of a river on the eastern coast of Imnas. Enchanted levitation stone holds it aloft, its enchantments woven from Enhancement, Telekinesis, and many other spheres that the city's mages maintain. Space aloft is limited, so its true population is small, and it is a refined and exclusive city of mages, home to the university and the council.

The city stands over a leyline most of the time. Leylines shift, and the mages of Sireth watch the current beneath their city closely whenever it begins to drift.
