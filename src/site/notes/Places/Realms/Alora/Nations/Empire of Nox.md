# Empire of Nox

## Overview

The Empire of Nox holds the west of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]], on the lands of the fallen Empire of Adriacium. It was raised from Adriacium's ruins in 5267 by [[Vera\|Vera]], one of the adventurers of [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Retribution\|Retribution]], who has stood at its head for 170 years. Her title, Chaos Lord, belongs to no other ruler in Alora, and the world has no better word for what she has become.

Nox is an empire in name and a council in practice. Vera holds almost no formal power and leaves the governing of the empire to a council of seven, while her presence and her reputation give Nox a weight among its neighbors that no other Wildlands nation can match. For half of every year she is not in the empire at all.

## Geography

Nox stretches from the western coast of the continent to its border with the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Republic of Korthak\|Republic of Korthak]]. Temperate rainforest covers the north around Lincugus, giving way to temperate forest and then to taiga in the south. A peninsula of wetland reaches out from the southwest, and rivers run through fertile valleys toward the coasts.

A great bay reaches deep into Nox from the south. The Algar call it the *Adriac Gulf*, a name kept from the days of Adriacium, and its sheltered waters carry much of the empire's coastal trade.

Ruins of Adriacium remain across the empire, though many of its cities have been rebuilt over the past 170 years. A few demons from 5267 still roam the wilder country, keeping to the shadows and causing trouble whenever they surface.

## Government

Nox is governed by the Council of Seven. Its members are drawn from different walks of life across the empire, and each is a *logothete*, responsible for one section of government:

- **The Logothete of the Treasury** - taxes, coinage, and the imperial mines
- **The Logothete of the Law** - the courts and the legal codes
- **The Logothete of the Army** - the land forces and the border forts
- **The Logothete of the Sea** - the fleet and the harbors
- **The Logothete of the Service** - the officials of the empire, its roads, and its records
- **The Logothete of the Phylakes** - the wardens against planar threats
- **The Logothete of the Envoys** - relations with foreign powers

Each logothete runs their own section, but no logothete may make a sweeping decision alone. Any matter beyond the ordinary business of a section goes before the full council and passes by majority vote. When a seat falls empty, the remaining six choose its successor by majority, most often from among the empire's dignitaries.

The council governs through the service, the body of officials who carry out the empire's business. Its officials are chosen by examination, open to any free person, and they keep the empire's records, collect its taxes, maintain its roads, and run its courts under the direction of the logothetes.



### The Chaos Lord

Vera's authority is almost entirely informal. She holds no veto written into law, no command of the army, and no seat on the council. What she has is the weight of 170 years, the loyalty of the empire she built, and a reputation no one in Nox would care to test. The council governs, and it does so knowing she is there.

Vera is present in Nox for the first seven months of each year, and absent for the last seven. Where she goes is not publicly known. It is known only that she leaves the Material Plane, and that she has never failed to return. The council governs the same way whether she is present or not, and the Algar mark neither her departures nor her returns.

Nox has no plan of succession. Most of its people assume that if Vera were ever gone, she would want the council to carry on without her, and few care to discuss it further.

### The Title

"Chaos Lord" is a stand-in title for something the world has no proper name for. Vera is no longer mortal, though she is far from the power of a demon lord, and scholars who have studied her case describe her as a new kind of lesser deific being. The title comes from her chaotic nature and her devotion to freedom.

The name "Empire" is inherited from Adriacium. Nox keeps it as a projection of power, and its neighbors take it seriously.

## Law

Nox's law rests on the Eldar legal codes inherited from Adriacium, revised and expanded by the council. The Logothete of the Law oversees the courts, and the service staffs them. Algar law protects the freedoms of worship, speech, travel, and petition, and it forbids bonded labor in every form.

Magic that binds a will, calls on outsiders, or raises the dead falls under strict law. The Phylakes, the Algar heirs of the Eldar Wards of Binding, investigate planar threats and enforce those laws. Their work is taken seriously everywhere in Nox, and its people understand why the founder of their empire keeps the office at full strength.

## Military

Nox's army rivals Bornumia's legions in strength. Its core is the *tagmata*, professional regiments garrisoned at Lincugus and the larger cities, and each province raises its own forces to guard the borders and the roads. The army trains to fight demons as well as mortal enemies, and it hunts down the roaming demons that still trouble the wilder country.

The imperial fleet guards the western coast, the northern sea, and the Adriac Gulf. It protects Nox's coastal trade and keeps the gulf's waters secure.

The Phylakes work alongside both. When a planar threat appears, the Phylakes investigate it and the army supplies the force to deal with it.

## Economy

Nox restored and expanded the industries of Adriacium. Its copper mines produce ore and worked copper goods, and its quarries supply marble and limestone. Algar wines are prized across the Wildlands, and the vineyards of the north supply every neighbor. The river valleys produce a farming surplus, and the forests supply timber.

Nox imports iron and steel, manufactured goods, textiles and fine clothing, exotic spices, and precision tools and instruments.

## Peoples and Culture

Nox is the heartland of [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Algar\|Algar]] culture, which also reaches across the eastern border into northwestern Korthak. The Algar are the most varied people in the Wildlands. [[Species and Cultures/Races/Daemedi\|Daemedi]] are the most common by a wide margin, [[Species and Cultures/Races/Human\|Humans]] and [[Species and Cultures/Races/Vullukk\|Vullukk]] are common, and members of every other race live among them.

After 5267, outsiders who had lived peaceably under the faceless emperor and would swear to Nox's laws were allowed to remain, and their descendants are part of Algar life today.

That allowance has since closed. Those who remain are given the benefit of the doubt, but the council has made it clear that no new outsiders will be permitted to settle in Nox.

## Relations

Nox holds more soft power than any of its neighbors, and it uses it through reputation and quiet pressure. Few rulers in the Wildlands care to cross the empire that Vera leads, and the council has learned that a quiet word from Lincugus often achieves more than an army could.

Korthak feels that pressure most. Casta counts have sought Vera's favor, Barbois politicians warn against it, and the Consuls tread carefully in every dealing with Nox. The [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Cilomutesan Union\|Cilomutesan Union]] deals readily with Nox's council and watches Vera's long reign with unease. The [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Empire of Bornumia]] respects Nox's army and its role in 5267, and the two great powers of the continent keep a cautious distance.

## History

Before 5267 these lands were the heart of the Empire of Adriacium. In 5167 Adriacium's emperor was murdered, and a faceless emperor rose in his place, who ruled for a century by a single law and let every other restraint fall away. In 5267 the faceless emperor's army of demons and devils marched out to war, and Adriacium fell with it.

Vera and her companions of Retribution fought that army, brought reinforcements from across the ocean, and banished [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Rouge, the Red Progenitor Demon\|Rouge]]. Adriacium did not survive the war. Its people were killed or scattered, and other powers rose in the ruins to claim its lands. Vera already commanded troops and influence from the war, and she was the strongest force in the region from the start. Within a few years her rivals had been absorbed or defeated, and the Empire of Nox had taken shape on Adriacium's western lands.

Over the following decades Nox rebuilt Adriacium's cities, roads, and mines and won full recognition from the powers of Alora. It has been stable for most of its 170 years, under the same Chaos Lord and the council she set beneath her.

## Capital

Lincugus, population 19,961, stands on the western coast amid the temperate rainforest of the north. It is the seat of the Council of Seven, the center of the service, and the home of the Eleutheria, the great festival of freedom held there at the turn of each new year.
