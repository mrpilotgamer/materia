# Empire of Bornumia

## Overview

The Empire of Bornumia holds the eastern half of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]], well over half the continent, and it is the heartland of [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Eldar\|Eldar]] culture. It has been an empire since around 4937, and its emperors rule from Conatagus, one of the most populous cities in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Alora\|Alora]]. Its legions and forts are among the strongest military forces on the continent.

In 5267 an army of demons and devils broke through Bornumia's western border and pushed deep into the empire before it was stopped. Bornumia survived where Adriacium and Presium did not, though it lost its mountain lands in the war. It has spent the years since rebuilding its strength and learning to rely on itself.

## Geography

Bornumia stretches from the mountain range in the west to the far eastern tip of the continent. Temperate deciduous forest runs along its northern coast, where Conatagus stands and most of its people live. Taiga covers the south, growing colder toward a coast bound in sea ice, and a long southern peninsula ends in tundra. Stretches of grassland lie in the west and east, among the empire's best farmland.

A great bay opens from the south and reaches deep into the empire, nearly cutting its eastern lobe away from the rest. The Eldar call it the *Mare Clausum*, the Closed Sea. Its sheltered waters carry much of the traffic between the eastern lobe and the heartland, and its shores are dotted with fishing towns.

The western border runs through the continent's only mountain range, home to the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Valekai\|Valekai]]. Before 5267 Bornumia held the mountainous lands around the range. It holds only the eastern foothills now, the country where Eldar culture was born.

The barrier between the planes is thin everywhere in Bornumia, and it shifts. A place that is safe one year may be dangerous the next, and no part of the empire is free of demon sightings for long.

## Government

Bornumia is an imperial autocracy. The emperor holds supreme authority over law, the legions, and the provinces, and the throne passes by dynasty. The present emperor is Lucius Valerius Corvus, a Daemedi of the Valerian dynasty who has reigned since around 5417.



The Senate of Conatagus advises the emperor, debates imperial law, and oversees the treasury. Its senators are drawn from the great Eldar familiae and from citizens who have held high office. The emperor can overrule the Senate, but emperors who ignore it too often find their reigns troubled.

Each province is governed by an imperial governor appointed by the emperor, who commands the province's garrison and hears its gravest cases. Beneath the governors, each town governs its own affairs. Its citizens elect their magistrates each year in the comitia, and former magistrates sit for life on the town's curia.

## Law

Bornumian law rests on the Eldar legal codes, the oldest written law in the Wildlands, with imperial edicts layered on top. Town magistrates hear most cases, governors hear appeals and capital crimes, and the emperor is the final court.

The Wards of Binding hold a place in Bornumian law that they hold nowhere else. Every town of any size keeps a Ward, a magistrate charged with knowledge of planar entities, who has authority to investigate demonic activity, seal dangerous sites, and call on the local garrison. Since 5267 the office has been taken with deadly seriousness, and its holders are among the most respected officials in any town.

## Military

Bornumia has one of the strongest militaries on the continent. Its army is organized into legions of professional soldiers, recruited from citizens and from non-citizens who earn citizenship through service. A chain of forts guards the western border and the roads of the interior, and every legion trains to fight demons as well as mortal enemies.

The imperial fleet is based on the northern coast and on the Mare Clausum. It guards the coasts, keeps the sea lanes open to the eastern lobe, and escorts trade along the northern sea, while the ice-bound southern coast needs little guarding.

Demon sightings keep the legions busy in peacetime. Garrisons answer the calls of the Wards, and a legion may be sent to wherever the barrier has thinned.

## Economy

In the years after 5267, Bornumia set out to depend on no one. It expanded farming in its grasslands and river valleys, built granaries in every province, and cleared land in the northern forests. Today it feeds itself in most years, though farming near the thinnest places in the barrier remains dangerous.

Bornumia exports high-quality hardwood from its forests, timber and furs from the taiga, and rare medicinal herbs that grow only in planar-touched soil. Demon-touched iron, ore strengthened by planar energy, is its rarest export, mined in small and dangerous quantities. The empire also sells the services of its engineers and its demon-hunters. It imports wine from the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Empire of Nox]], salt and ships' goods from the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Cilomutesan Union\|Cilomutesan Union]], and luxuries from across Alora.

## Peoples and Culture

Bornumia is the heartland of Eldar culture, the only nation where it remains dominant. Its people are mostly [[Species and Cultures/Races/Daemedi\|Daemedi]] and [[Species and Cultures/Races/Human\|Humans]], in roughly equal numbers, and it has the highest density of Daemedi anywhere in Alora. Members of other races live in the cities in small numbers.

The Valekai live in the mountains along the western border and trade with the Eldar of the foothills. They pay little heed to Bornumian law, and so far the empire has not found it worth the trouble to press the matter.

## Relations

Bornumia's relations with the three western nations are workable but strained. The Eldar regard the western peoples as cousins, and trade and travel between them are steady. Some Bornumian senators speak of the western cultures as lost branches that should return to the Eldar fold, and the western nations hear that talk clearly.

Its closest and most delicate relationship is with the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Cilomutesan Union\|Cilomutesan Union]], its only neighbor by land. The two share a border through the mountain range, and both count the range within their borders. The Union holds the western slopes that Bornumia lost in the war, and some in Conatagus have never accepted the loss.

## History

Eldar culture was born in the eastern foothills, and it spread west across the continent during the Age of Expansion. The empires of that age rotted from within, and the Eldar still point to that decay as the great lesson of their history. The Empire of Bornumia rose from the Eldar heartland around 4937 and claims to be the heir of those older realms, and a succession of dynasties has ruled it ever since.

For a century before 5267, Bornumia shared the continent with Adriacium under its faceless emperor. The empire held its western border closed as far as it could, and it watched the lawlessness beyond with alarm.

In 5267 the army that marched out of Adriacium broke through Bornumia's western border and pushed past the mountains into the heartland. The legions could not stop it alone. [[Vera\|Vera]] and her party, [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Retribution\|Retribution]], pressed the threat the faceless emperor posed to all of Alora on the powers across the ocean, and brought back reinforcements, chiefly from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/The Rayhostian Theocracy\|the Rayhostian Theocracy]] and from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imnas\|Imnas]] in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Azure Expanse\|The Azure Expanse]]. Together they halted the army inside Bornumia, and when they banished [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Rouge, the Red Progenitor Demon\|Rouge]] the army broke apart.

Bornumia survived, but it lost its hold on the mountainous lands around the range. With its legions spent and its western provinces ravaged, it could not reclaim them, and the Union's frontier came to rest there in the decades after. The empire spent those decades rebuilding its towns, its legions, and its granaries, and it has emerged as strong as it has been in centuries.

The present emperor, Lucius Valerius Corvus, took the throne around 5417.

## Capital

Conatagus, population 50,413, stands near the northern coast in the heart of the empire. It is one of the most populous cities in Alora, the seat of the emperor and the Senate, and the center of Eldar learning and law.
