# Laurland

## Overview

Laurland is an island nation in the sea between [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Jotuun Nexus\|The Jotuun Nexus]] and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]], and one of the great trade powers of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Alora\|Alora]]. It is ruled by its merchant houses, whose ships carry goods to nearly every coast in Alora and whose navy guards the routes they sail. Its people are [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Dali\|Dali]], mostly [[Species and Cultures/Races/Grendal\|Grendals]] and [[Species and Cultures/Races/Chisana\|Chisana]], and Laurland's brokers are known wherever goods change hands for their skill at haggling a price.

## Geography

Laurland is a single broad island, flat and forested across its whole breadth. Its northern shore is long and broken by small bays and headlands, while the west, east, and south run smoother. A shallow shelf surrounds the whole island before the sea floor drops away. The capital stands on the north coast at the head of a bay, where a river flows north out of the interior. A second river rises in the east and runs out to the eastern shore. Laurland shares no border with any nation, and the nearest mainland lies across open water in every direction.

## Environment

Forest covers nearly all of Laurland. Temperate rainforest fills the north, the east, and most of the interior, kept green by rain off the surrounding sea. Across the west and southwest, and in scattered stretches of the interior, it gives way to drier temperate forest of broadleaf trees. A small wetland lies at the island's southern tip. The land is low and level throughout, with no hills of note.

The climate is mild on the northern coast and grows warmer toward the south, where the island reaches toward the hot latitudes. Winters are gentle everywhere, and the sea keeps the whole island from extremes of heat or cold.

Laurland's forests supply hardwoods prized across Alora for fine building and furniture, and its interior grows spices found nowhere else. The sea gives more than the land does. The fishing grounds on the shelf are rich, the shallows hold deep beds of pearl oysters, the warmer southern waters grow coral, and evaporation ponds along the coast yield a high-quality sea salt.

## Government

Laurland is governed by the Tojjar, a council of the heads of its great merchant houses. The council meets in Felavik, makes the nation's law, sets its tariffs, and commands its navy. The Tojjar has no single head, and its members decide matters together, often after long bargaining among themselves.

A seat on the Tojjar belongs to a house for as long as the house can keep its share of the fleet at sea. A house that falls behind in ships and crews loses its seat, and a rising house that can outfit its share may claim one. The Dali keep no hereditary nobility, and Laurland's oligarchy follows the same principle: a house holds power through its wealth and its ships, and birth alone guarantees it nothing.

Five houses hold seats on the Tojjar today:

- **House Daryani,** led by Behrouz Daryani
- **House Morvaridi,** led by Manijeh Morvaridi
- **House Zarrabi,** led by Omid Zarrabi
- **House Bazargani,** led by Firouzeh Bazargani
- **House Nakhodazadeh,** led by Rostam Nakhodazadeh

## Law

The Tojjar makes the law of the island, and much of it concerns trade: tariffs, debts, harbor rights, and the conduct of ships at sea. Local affairs are left to the anjomans of each town and village, which settle disputes and oversee the markets as they do across the Dali world. Debts are taken seriously, and a house that defaults on one loses standing in every harbor Laurland ships visit.

## Military

Laurland's navy is among the most powerful in Alora. It belongs to the state, but the great houses build, supply, and crew it, each providing its share of ships as the price of its seat on the Tojjar. The fleet escorts Laurland's merchant ships, keeps the sea lanes clear of pirates, and covers much of the coastal defense of its ally [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Khania\|Khania]].

Laurland keeps no field army. Its soldiers serve aboard the fleet as landing parties, trained to come ashore quickly, strike, and return to their ships before any defense can gather. They are used against pirate harbors, raiders, and anyone who threatens a Laurland convoy, and they have never been meant to hold ground.

## Economy

Trade is Laurland's lifeblood. Its merchant fleet links nearly every coast in Alora, and Laurland ships carry goods between nations that rarely trade with one another directly. The island buys far more than it needs and sells the surplus onward. It takes timber and furs from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Hovden\|Hovden]] well beyond its own use and resells them across the world's trade routes.

Laurland also sells its skill at the bargaining table. Its brokers, the dallals, are hired by foreign merchants and trading companies to negotiate prices on their behalf. Khanian mediators settle disputes between nations, while a Laurland dallal is hired to win the better price. By Dali custom, a dallal may outwit the other side of a deal by any means the bazaar allows, but owes complete honesty to the client who hired them.

The island feeds itself on fish, spices, and its own produce, and it supplies food to others as well. It has long been [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Narathatath\|Narathatath]]'s main supplier, sending its own fish and spices alongside grain and livestock bought on the mainland and carried across. Its other exports are pearls and pearl jewelry, fine hardwoods, sea salt, coral, and preserved fish. Laurland imports metals and ores, stone and building materials, manufactured goods, and textiles.

## Peoples and Culture

Laurland is wholly [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Dali\|Dali]] in culture. Most of its people are [[Species and Cultures/Races/Grendal\|Grendals]], with a large [[Species and Cultures/Races/Chisana\|Chisana]] population. Laurland's Dali lean toward the sea, the ship, and the trading voyage, while their Khanian kin keep more of the life of the house and garden. Pearl diving is a Laurland tradition, and Laurland sailors and merchants are a common sight aboard trading ships across Alora.

## Relations

Laurland keeps a formal alliance with [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Khania\|Khania]] that dates from Khania's founding in 5117, and its navy kept [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Helano\|Helano]]'s armies away from the Dali coast. It has long fed [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Narathatath\|Narathatath]] and is [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Hovden\|Hovden]]'s largest customer. The closing of the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Kingdom of Stamvia\|Kingdom of Stamvia]] in 5436 cost Laurland a market, and its ships no longer call at Stamvian harbors.

## History

Laurland was once [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Yotuun\|Yotuun]] in culture, its harbors founded by seafarers from the Nexus. As its trade with the Khanian coast grew over the generations, Dali customs took hold, and the island gradually became Dali. Many of its towns and harbors still bear Yotuun names, the capital among them.

When the city-states of the east united as Khania in 5117, Laurland allied with the new republic. Its fleet held the coast while [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Helano\|Helano]] pressed from the west, and Helano never reached the Dali shore. Laurland came through the upheavals of 5267 untouched, and it has prospered on trade in the 170 years since.

## Capital

Felavik, population 31,640, stands on the northern coast at the head of a bay, where the island's main river meets the sea. It is the seat of the Tojjar and Laurland's greatest harbor, and ships from across Alora crowd its docks. Its name is Yotuun, kept from the island's earlier days.
