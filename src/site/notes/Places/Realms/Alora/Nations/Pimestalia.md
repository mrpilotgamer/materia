# Pimestalia

<!-- GAP: The current Presidente of the Junta General is unnamed. -->

## Overview

Pimestalia is a federal republic holding the centre of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Azure Expanse\|The Azure Expanse]], founded in 5240 when its people broke away from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imestalia\|Imestalia]] in civil war. Its founders charged that the nobles of Imestalia were abusing their power, and they built a nation without titles, governed by elected officials and the sworn citizens of its provinces. Its people are [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Portizian\|Portizian]], sharing their culture with the rival they left. Pimestalia fields the larger army of the two, and its iron feeds the forges of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Yoichizu\|Yoichizu]].

## Geography

Pimestalia lies in the centre of the continent, between Imestalia and Morlas Bay. The mountain range runs along its western border, and Pimestalia holds the eastern slopes and their mines, with grassland around their foot. East of the mountains the land is forest, temperate deciduous and temperate rainforest, cut by rivers running to the bay. In the north the forest turns tropical, and a peninsula reaches northeast into the bay toward Imnas.

Pimestalia's only coast faces Morlas Bay, the neutral waters it shares with [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imnas\|Imnas]] and Yoichizu. It borders Imnas to the north, Imestalia to the west along the mountains, and Yoichizu to the south and southeast.

## Government

Pimestalia is a federation of provinces. Every sworn citizen, every vecino of a town who has sworn to its fuero, holds a vote, and no title of nobility is recognized anywhere in the nation.

Each province sends elected delegates, the *procuradores*, to the *Junta General*, the national assembly that meets at Freygbutumn. The Junta makes national law, sets taxes, and elects the *Presidente*, who leads the government for a fixed term and answers to the Junta for every decision. Within each province, an elected provincial junta governs local affairs, and the towns govern themselves through their concejos and elected alcaldes.

## Law

Each town's fuero belongs to the town alone, and Pimestalians guard theirs fiercely. National law is made by the Junta and binds every province. The highest judge is the *Justicia Mayor*, appointed by the Junta for life, whose duty is to defend the rights of citizens against any official who would abuse them, the Presidente included.

## Military

Pimestalia fields a larger land army than Imestalia, raised from its provinces and commanded under the Junta. Its navy is the smaller of the two and keeps to Morlas Bay.

The mountains along the western border are impassable to any large force. They grow lower toward their northern and southern ends, and those lowlands are the only ground where armies can cross, so the civil war stalled there. Pimestalian patrols watch the passes for raiders, smugglers, and small bands, and they are heaviest at the two ends of the range, where Imestalian patrols watch from the other side.

## Economy

Pimestalia's wealth lies in its mountains. The eastern slopes yield coal, iron ore, and limestone, and most of the iron is sold to Yoichizu, whose smiths have little of their own. Wool and cloth from the Mesta's flocks are its other great trade, and Freygbutumn's harbor carries Pimestalian goods across Morlas Bay and beyond.

Pimestalia imports luxuries, fine manufactured goods, and the raw materials it lacks. Its rivalry with Imestalia is fought largely in the market: the two compete for the same buyers, and trade disputes between them are common.

## Peoples and Culture

Pimestalia is Portizian throughout, and like Imestalia it counts many races among its people, none of them a majority. It reads the Portizian values toward liberty: the fuero as rights no lord may take, and honor bound up with loyalty to one's neighbors and cause. Pimestalians address one another as vecino or vecina, dress plainly as a point of principle, and keep the founding of their nation as a great festival. A few isolated [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Dulandi\|Dulandi]] villages lie in the tropical forest along the northern border.

## Relations

Pimestalia's rivalry with Imestalia shapes its foreign policy, fought now in markets and trade disputes. Its iron trade gives it a hold on Yoichizu, which needs the ore for its forges. It trades with Imnas, whose schools train many Pimestalian mages.

## History

In 5240 the eastern lands of Imestalia rose against the crown. Their leaders charged that the nobles were abusing their power and causing strife, and they demanded that power be spread among the people. They broke away and founded Pimestalia as a federation without titles.

The civil war lasted about five years. Neither side could force a decisive blow across the mountains, and when the war reached a stalemate, the two signed a treaty that left the border along the range.

In 5267, when the army of demons and devils marched out of Adriacium, [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Retribution\|Retribution]] reached Pimestalia, and Pimestalia sent an advisor to the Wildlands to aid them. She was Seki Thundersword, unknown to history beyond her name, said to have been a strong soldier on the Pimestalian side of the civil war.

## Capital

Freygbutumn, population 56,009, is a port on Morlas Bay and the seat of the Junta General. It is the chief harbor of the bay, facing Imnas and Yoichizu across the neutral waters, and the iron of the eastern slopes leaves for Yoichizu's forges from its docks.
