# Kingdom of Stamvia

## Overview

The Kingdom of Stamvia holds the western grasslands of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Jotuun Nexus\|The Jotuun Nexus]]. It was once a republic with a fleet strong enough that Helano conquered it to take the ships. Since winning independence in 5267, it has become a feudal kingdom under the Valor family. In 5436 King Gerrick Valor killed his brother and seized the throne, and this year the kingdom fell into civil war. Gerrick has closed the kingdom to all foreign trade, and few outside its borders can say how the war stands.

## Geography

Stamvia holds the broad western lobe of the continent. Flat grassland covers the west, ideal for herding, and gives way to savanna in the east, where the land meets the edge of the central desert. The equator runs almost exactly through the capital, Sannes, on the northern coast. Natural harbors and coastal forests line the shores and have supplied the kingdom's shipyards for centuries. Stamvia borders [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Hovden\|Hovden]] to the northeast and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Khania\|Khania]] to the southeast.

## Government

The Valor family was a respected political house of the old republic. When the republic began to crumble in the turmoil after Helano's fall, the Valors moved quickly to take power. They swept the republic away and built a feudal kingdom in its place, arguing that the nation needed efficiency and stability more than it needed assemblies. Many Stamvians agreed at the time, since the country was close to collapse. Beneath the crown stood the noble houses, headed by jarlar who held land from the king.

That order has now been largely destroyed. Since seizing the throne, Gerrick Valor has ruled through army officers and loyal soldiers, and more than nine in ten of the noble houses that stood before the coup have been wiped out.

## Law

Before the coup, the king and the jarlar made and enforced the law, each lord judging the people of his own lands. Local þings continued to meet under Yotuun custom and settled most disputes among the kynnar, though the crown held the final word.

Stamvia is now under martial law. Army officers carry out Gerrick's orders directly, and the local þings meet only where the fighting and the officers allow.

## Military

The army is split between the king and his opponents, and the lines between them keep shifting. Gerrick holds the entire navy. The fleet is a shadow of what Helano once coveted, having declined through corruption and mismanagement across two centuries, but no one else in the war has any ships at all.

## Economy

Herding is the backbone of the kingdom. The grasslands support large herds of cattle and sheep, which supply meat, wool, hides, and leather. Horses were once far more common, but Helano took great numbers of them during the protectorate, and the herds have never recovered.

Quarrying and stonemasonry have long been major industries, though they have declined in recent years. Shipbuilding remains a large industry even as the navy itself was allowed to decay, and the harbors also supply seafood and maritime services. Before the coup, Stamvia imported iron and metal fittings for its ships, hardwood for masts, rope, and sailcloth.

After seizing the throne, Gerrick closed the kingdom to foreign trade entirely, halting imports and exports alike. The shipyards now work with whatever materials remain inside the borders, and military equipment has become an urgent need that no merchant can legally fill. The Great Desert Road has suffered badly for the loss of its western traffic.

## Peoples and Culture

Stamvia is [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Yotuun\|Yotuun]] in culture. Its grassland Yotuun are more mobile and trade-focused than their desert kin, which traditionalists criticize as softening. The population is roughly half [[Species and Cultures/Races/Yol-Mar\|Yol-Mar]], about two-fifths [[Species and Cultures/Races/Human\|Human]], and a tenth other races. The Yol-Mar were common here long before Helano fell, and many of Helano's refugees joined them after 5267.

Many Stamvian kynnar move with their herds through the year, shifting between the western grasslands and the eastern savanna as the rains come and go. They live in portable halls of hide and timber that can be packed onto carts, and each kynnar keeps a permanent höll in a home village where it gathers for the winter þing. Seasonal cattle fairs bring the wandering kynnar together to trade, settle disputes, and arrange marriages. Since the coup, martial law and the fighting have stifled the migrations, and many herds now stay close to home.

## History

Before Helano, Stamvia was a republic governed by an assembly of the kynnar, a national þing that chose its leaders by vote. Its harbors and coastal timber gave it the finest shipyards in the Nexus, and its stonemasons were famous across the continent.

Helano subjugated Stamvia in 5067, seeking both its stonemasons and its navy. The republic kept its internal government as a protectorate, while the Khan took ships, craftsmen, and horses. The fleet began its long decline in the protectorate's later years and never recovered.

Stamvia regained its independence in 5267, but the republic began to crumble almost at once. The Valor family took power, established the kingdom, and brought the country through the worst of the turmoil. It struggled in the generations that followed, and corruption and mismanagement hollowed out the navy.

In 5436 Gerrick Valor killed his brother, King Adenia Valor, and seized the throne. The coup came suddenly, with no known warning signs. Gerrick claimed that his brother was too weak and merciful to rule, and his supporters say Adenia was too soft militarily, inviting provocation from desert raiders and neighboring nations. Gerrick then turned on the nobility and destroyed most of its houses.

Civil war broke out in 5437. Prince Alexander Valor, Adenia's heir, vanished after the coup and was not seen again until the war began. He now leads the members of the crown who remained loyal to his father, among them Jarl Magnus, against the king. The war is changing fast, and its course is still unclear.

## Capital

Sannes stands on the northern coast, almost exactly on the equator. It is the traditional seat of the crown, though who holds it at any given moment depends on the war. Its population is unknown, since executions, conscription, and the lack of any census since the coup have left no reliable count.

## Pronunciation

Stamvia (STAM-vee-uh), Sannes (SAN-ess)
