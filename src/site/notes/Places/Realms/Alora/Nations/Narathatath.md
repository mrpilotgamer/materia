# Narathatath

## Overview

Narathatath holds the western continent of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]], and it is ruled by dragons. A council of ten dragons governs as a collective monarch, and dragons commonly fly through its major cities. Its people are mostly [[Species and Cultures/Races/Drakmed\|Drakmed]], who are believed to be the humanoid descendants of dragons, and its dominant culture is [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Drako\|Drako]].

Myth holds that the nation was founded by [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Riavexect, God of Want\|Riavexect, God of Want]], before the Great Schism War. The council has ruled for thousands of years, and some of its present members were alive when the Human Uprising of 4287 broke dragon rule over the eastern continent.

## Geography

Narathatath's continent is mostly mountainous and actively volcanic, and a narrow isthmus joins its northwestern lobe to the main landmass. The capital, Kisondarket, stands on the western coast near that isthmus. Its southernmost islands lie near the equator, and a narrow strait to the east separates it from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/The Rayhostian Theocracy\|The Rayhostian Theocracy]].

Sulfur springs rise throughout the volcanic territory, and the deep mines follow the ore veins of the mountains. Farmable land is scarce, and the harsh environment keeps the population smaller than in gentler regions.

## Government

The council of ten rules as a collective monarch, and no single dragon on it holds more sway than another. Decisions pass by majority vote, and a tied council continues to debate. If no decision can be reached in reasonable time, it calls in an odd number of elder dragons from outside the council to break the tie.

No law requires a particular balance of dragons on the council, but tradition has almost always kept each seat for a different kind of dragon, five chromatic and five metallic.

**Chromatic**
- **Vraxthaleon the Ironforge**, an ancient red dragon, military commander
- **Nethysara the Stormcaller**, an ancient blue dragon, strategic planner
- **Kryzeleth the Eternal**, an ancient white dragon, advocate for tradition
- **Vorthak the Whisperwind**, an ancient green dragon, handler of internal politics
- **Ozrethys the Vaultwarden**, an ancient black dragon, keeper of the dragon-guarded vaults

**Metallic**
- **Aertharion the Shieldbreaker**, an ancient bronze dragon, military commander
- **Jocularn the Truthseeker**, an ancient copper dragon, overseer of justice and law
- **Solanneth the Wise**, a gold dragon more than 3,000 years old and the most respected of the ten
- **Miralyth the Coinweaver**, an ancient silver dragon, manager of trade relations
- **Chalcyon the Lorekeeper**, an ancient brass dragon, overseer of cultural affairs

Beneath the council, each hold is ruled by its dragon as a local government over the territory that dragon has claimed, and the dragons of the holds answer to the council.

## Law

Jocularn the Truthseeker oversees justice and law for the council. Narathatath's laws are engraved, following the Drako practice of setting down in metal what must endure. Ordinary cases are judged by the dragon of the hold where they arise, and matters between holds, or those touching a dragon, go before the council.

## Military

Command is shared between two councillors, Vraxthaleon the Ironforge and Aertharion the Shieldbreaker, one chromatic and one metallic. Each draws most readily on the holds of their own kind, and any war of real size requires the two to cooperate.

Drakmed make up most of the ground soldiery, while dragons carry great weight in any battle they join. Narathatath had no navy until recently, when one was established with the Trox of its eastern shores.

The Crimson Circuit, the trade route linking the major cities of the region, is heavily defended against bandits and wild monsters. Its garrisons once watched for the Theocracy as well, but that concern has faded as tensions have eased.

## Economy

Narathatath is rich in metal and stone, with mines yielding diamonds, rubies, and emeralds. Gold and silver run throughout the territory, and the sulfur springs supply both alchemists and armies. Its iron ore comes from ancient [[Species and Cultures/Races/Trox\|Trox]] mines and is counted the highest quality in the world's trade, and coal supplies fuel for forging and heating.

The nation exports masterwork weapons and armor made under dragon supervision, along with high-grade steel, refined ores and precious metals, and cut gems and jewelry. Military equipment, siege engines, and military consulting are sold abroad as well, and dragon-guarded vaults offer storage and security for high-value goods.

Food is among Narathatath's most pressing needs. Scarce farmland and the dragons' demand for variety leave it dependent on imports, and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Laurland\|Laurland]] has long been its main supplier. Recent trade deals have added imports from the Theocracy. It also imports exotic spices, fine textiles and silk, rare books, precision instruments, and wooden goods.

## Peoples and Culture

[[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Drako\|Drako]] culture is dominant throughout Narathatath, and most of its people are Drakmed, organized into holds under the dragons they serve. Trox live in large numbers along the eastern shores, some of them having come up from beneath the ground, and along those coasts the more common culture is [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Trow\|Trow]]. Humans are much rarer, and few have remained since the uprising.

The dragons worship Riavexect as the first dragon and their ancestor, and the god is the patron of the nation, with a major temple in Kisondarket. [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Alfugel, God of Hatred\|Alfugel]] is also widely worshiped, particularly among the Trox.

## History

Myth holds that Riavexect founded the nation before the Great Schism War. The council of ten has ruled for thousands of years, and in that time dragon rule once reached across both continents of the Crimson Lands.

In 4287 the Human Uprising broke dragon rule on the eastern continent and founded the Rayhostian Theocracy. Narathatath remembers the uprising as a betrayal. The centuries that followed were marked by tension with the Theocracy, though open conflict between the two nations was rare.

The two nations have kept an extended peace for the past 170 years. In that time they have established multiple trade treaties through the diplomatic efforts of Slyrsomyr the Bridgemaker, a young adult black dragon and Narathatath's chief diplomat to the Theocracy. Relations have improved over the past century, though the two nations remain wary of each other.

## Capital

Kisondarket, population 11,427, stands on the western coast near the isthmus. It is the seat of the council and the home of Riavexect's major temple.

## Pronunciation

Narathatath (nahr-ATH-ah-TATH), Kisondarket (kee-SAWN-dawr-KET)
