# Imestalia



## Overview

Imestalia is a kingdom holding the southwest of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Azure Expanse\|The Azure Expanse]], and the older of the two [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Portizian\|Portizian]] nations. Its people are of many races sharing one culture, which began on its coast long ago. In 5240 it lost its eastern lands to civil war, when [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Pimestalia\|Pimestalia]] broke away, and the crown has held firm to the old order ever since. Imestalia is the richer of the two rivals and holds the stronger navy, and its capital, Quichollow, is the most populous capital in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Alora\|Alora]] on record.

## Geography

Imestalia holds the southwest of the continent, with a long coastline on the west and south. Most of the country is temperate deciduous forest, cut by rivers whose fertile valleys feed the nation. The continent's only mountain range runs along its eastern border, with grassland around its foot and a small patch of taiga at its southern end. In the north, near the border with [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imnas\|Imnas]], the forest turns tropical.

Imestalia borders Imnas to the north, Pimestalia to the east along the mountains, and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Yoichizu\|Yoichizu]] to the southeast. Quichollow faces a narrow strait to the northwest, across which lie [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]].

## Government

Imestalia is ruled by a king, who governs with the advice and consent of the *Cortes*, the kingdom's assembly. The Cortes gathers three estates: the great nobles, the clergy of [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Arloya, God of Devotion\|Arloya]], and the *procuradores* sent by the chartered towns. The king summons it to approve new taxes and to swear allegiance to each new heir, and its members may bring grievances before the crown.

The crown passes by inheritance within the royal family. Beneath the king, the great noble houses hold lands and offices, and the lesser nobles, the *hidalgos*, serve as officers, magistrates, and courtiers. Each town is governed by its concejo under a fuero granted by the crown or its lord, and the crown or the lord confirms the town's alcaldes.

The civil war did not change the crown's course. Faced with the loss of its eastern lands and the charge that its nobles abused their power, the monarchy held firm to its order, and Imestalians who share its view hold that the war proved the need for it.

## Law

Law in Imestalia flows from the crown. Each town keeps its fuero, but the king's law stands above them all, and the highest court is the *Real Audiencia*, the royal court of appeal at Quichollow. Its judges are appointed by the king and hear cases the towns and the lords cannot settle.

## Military

Imestalia keeps the stronger navy of the two Portizian nations, based at Quichollow and the harbors of its long coast. Its land army is smaller than Pimestalia's, and much of it is raised from the retinues of the great nobles. Many of its weapons are bought from Yoichizu, forged from iron that Yoichizu buys from Pimestalia.

The mountains along the eastern border are impassable to any large force, though they grow lower toward their northern and southern ends. Imestalian patrols watch the passes for raiders, smugglers, and small bands, and they are heaviest in those lowlands at either end, where Pimestalian patrols watch from the other side.

## Economy

Imestalia has the stronger economy of the two rivals. The river valleys grow grain and other crops in abundance, the grasslands and valleys raise cattle, sheep, and horses, and the forests supply timber. Its mines yield copper, tin, lead, and some iron. Wool from the Mesta's flocks feeds a large cloth trade, and the rivers and the long coast carry goods through Quichollow to the wider world.

Imestalia imports weapons, chiefly from Yoichizu, along with fine manufactured goods, medicines, and luxuries. Its rivalry with Pimestalia is fought largely through trade: the two compete for the same markets, and disputes over tariffs and trade rights between them are common.

## Peoples and Culture

Imestalia is Portizian throughout, and its people include [[Species and Cultures/Races/Human\|Humans]], [[Species and Cultures/Races/Mannee\|Mannee]], [[Species and Cultures/Races/Araki\|Araki]], [[Species and Cultures/Races/Daemedi\|Daemedi]], and many others, none of them a majority. The kingdom reads the Portizian values toward order: the fuero as law granted from above, and honor bound up with loyalty to the crown. Nobles are addressed as don or doña, and the old families take pride in tracing their lines to the first settlers of the coast. A few isolated [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Dulandi\|Dulandi]] villages lie in the tropical forest along the northern border.

## Relations

Imestalia's rivalry with Pimestalia is the fact of its foreign policy, fought in markets and trade disputes now and no longer on the battlefield. It buys its weapons from Yoichizu and trades with Imnas, whose schools train many Imestalian mages. Quichollow's harbor looks across the strait toward the Crimson Lands.

## History

Portizian culture began on the coast around Quichollow, among humans who settled there long ago. Some historians believe they came from the Crimson Lands, but no record proves it. From the coast the culture spread inland, and the lands it reached became the kingdom of Imestalia.

In 5240 the kingdom split. Reformers in the east charged that the nobles were abusing their power and causing strife, and they broke away to found Pimestalia. The civil war lasted about five years. The mountains kept either side from striking the other a decisive blow, and when the war reached a stalemate, the two signed a treaty that left the border along the range.

## Capital

Quichollow, population 72,224, stands on the northwest coast, facing the strait toward the Crimson Lands. It is the most populous capital in Alora on record, the seat of the crown and the Cortes, and the home port of the navy. The city is where Portizian culture began, and its old quarters are the oldest Portizian streets anywhere.
