# Cilomutesan Union

## Overview

The Cilomutesan Union is a federation of ten [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Sonta\|Sonta]] cities in the centre of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]], on land that once belonged to the Oligarchy of Presium. Each city is a merchant republic that governs itself, and together they send their representatives to a federal council that meets at Diaquingis. The Union was founded 107 years ago, and it is the oldest of the republics that rose from Presium's ruins.

The Union is a nation of shipwrights, glassmakers, and traders, built for defense. Its cities guard their own walls in peace and join their strength under the council in war. Their watchmen, the Lords of the Night, hunt demons within the walls with a seriousness born of 5267.

## Geography

The Union is a narrow strip of land running from the northern coast to the southern, between the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Republic of Korthak\|Republic of Korthak]] to the west and the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Empire of Bornumia]] to the east. Temperate forest and a stretch of grassland cover its north, where nine of its ten cities stand, along the coast and the rivers that run down to it.

The eastern side of the Union rises into the continent's only mountain range. Its northernmost peaks, abandoned by the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Valekai\|Valekai]] in 5267, are worked by Sonta miners for stone and ore. The Union also holds the western slopes that Bornumia lost in the war, and the high passes beyond them belong to the Valekai in all but name.

South of the forests, taiga covers the land down to an ice-bound coast with no harbors. The south is wild and largely untamed, home to one landlocked city and little else.

## Government

Each of the ten cities governs itself as a republic, through its own *Consegio* and its own elected *dose*. The Union governs only what the cities share: defense, relations with foreign powers, the regulation of trade between the cities, and the settlement of disputes among them.

These shared matters belong to the *Consegio dei Diese*, the Council of Ten. Each city's Consegio elects one councillor to sit on it, and the council meets at Diaquingis. Its chair, the *Primo*, rotates among the ten cities each year, and leads the council's debates without holding any greater power than the other nine.

Sonta custom holds that those who govern may not wear masks, and the Council of Ten follows it strictly. Its councillors sit barefaced, debate in the open, and cast their votes aloud, even though citizens voting in their own city's Consegio do so masked. Most decisions pass by a simple majority, and a declaration of war requires seven votes.

## Law

Each city keeps its own laws, built on the Eldar legal codes that every Sonta city inherited. The Council of Ten makes federal law only on the matters the cities have given it, and it hears disputes between cities when they cannot settle them themselves. Contracts made in one city are honored in all ten.

Within the walls, the Lords of the Night enforce the law after dark and guard against demonic influence. Every citizen knows the watch may order any person to unmask when there is cause, and refusing is a crime in every city of the Union.

## Military

In peacetime each city keeps its own soldiers, as a city guard and a citizen militia, and each harbor city keeps its own ships, so the Union itself has no standing army.

In time of war the council calls the cities' forces together into a federal army and a federal fleet under its command. It appoints a captain-general to lead the army and an admiral to lead the fleet, each for the length of the war and no longer. The Sonta distrust any commander who keeps power after the fighting is done.

The Union's military is built for defense. City walls, harbor chains, and the federal fleet guard against raiders, and the Lords of the Night guard against demons. The Union has never fought a war of conquest, and its councillors are proud of it.

## Economy

The Union lives by its craftsmen and its harbors. Its arsenals turn out ships at a pace few nations can match, and naval vessels are among its leading exports, along with the services of Sonta crews and naval instructors. Fresh and preserved fish, salt from coastal evaporation ponds, and preserved foods round out its trade.

Sonta glasswork is prized across Alora: window glass, lamps, mirrors, beads, and fine painted glass. Masks of leather, lacquered paper, and glass are made in every city, and the finest are sought by collectors far beyond the Wildlands. The northern peaks yield stone and a modest amount of ore.

The Union imports iron and steel for its shipyards, hardwood for masts, rope and sailcloth, grain from inland, luxury goods, and navigation instruments.

## Peoples and Culture

The Union is the heartland of Sonta culture. Its people are [[Species and Cultures/Races/Human\|Humans]] and [[Species and Cultures/Races/Daemedi\|Daemedi]] in roughly equal numbers, followed by [[Species and Cultures/Races/Vullukk\|Vullukk]], with members of other races in small numbers. Sonta culture also reaches across the western border into the eastern edge of Korthak.

The Valekai live in the mountains along the Union's eastern side. They pay little heed to Union law, guide Sonta miners and merchants through their passes, and watch the mines on their old peaks with unease.

## Relations

Korthak is the Union's partner in trade and its old rival from the city-state wars. The two divided the lands of Presium between them, and a few old claims along their border are still argued over. The Sonta respect Korthak's republic and find its Casta lords poor company.

Bornumia is the Union's only other neighbor by land, and the relationship is delicate. The Union holds the western slopes that Bornumia lost in 5267, and some in Conatagus have never accepted the loss. The council keeps its eastern garrisons strong and its trade with Bornumia steady, hoping neither will be tested.

The Union watches the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Empire of Nox]] with wary respect. Its councillors deal with Nox's council readily, and the long reign of Vera sits uneasily with every Sonta instinct about power.

## History

Before 5267 the Union's lands belonged to the Oligarchy of Presium, a maritime power ruled by a closed circle of great merchant families. In 5267 an army of demons and devils swept across the continent, and Presium fell. The cities that survived fought one another for power in the decades that followed.

In the eastern half of the old oligarchy, the harbor cities that came through those wars turned away from rule by a few great families and built merchant republics in its place. Their shared ways became Sonta culture, and their shared enemies pushed them together.

In 5330, ten cities met at Diaquingis, at the mouth of the river Cilomutes, and swore the Oath of the Ten, binding themselves into a union for their common defense. Each city's dose cast a ring into the river, wedding their cities to one another and to the sea. The Union takes its name from that river, and the ten rings are remembered at every Marriage of the Sea.

The Union has held together for 107 years. It is older than the Republic of Korthak by seventeen years, and the Sonta never let their Korthak neighbors forget it.

## Capital

Diaquingis, population 12,428, stands at the mouth of the river Cilomutes on the northern coast. It is the meeting place of the Council of Ten and a harbor city in its own right, with its own Consegio, its own dose, and its own arsenal. The Union has no capital in the sense its neighbors use the word. Diaquingis holds no authority over the other nine cities, though it enjoys the prestige of the place where the Oath was sworn.
