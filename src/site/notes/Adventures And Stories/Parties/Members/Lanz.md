# Lanz

Lanz was the fighter of [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Retribution\|Retribution]]. He was Human, born in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Empire of Adriacium\|Adriacium]] during the Faceless Century, and he left soon after he came of age.

## Early Life

He grew up under the faceless emperor's single law, that no one may infringe on the freedom of another. The emperor enforced no other, so in practice a person's own strength was the only protection they had. Lanz left Adriacium knowing how to fight.

## In Retribution

He fought with his bare hands and carried no weapon at all. By the end of the war of 5267 his knuckles were scarred white and his fists were legend, and stories claim they could tear through space and time itself. Scholars dispute that, though nobody disputes that he was frightening to stand against.



## After the War

Lanz vanished after the war. Since then, tavern stories from Felavik to Conatagus have told of a bare-handed stranger with scarred knuckles who ends a fight that wasn't his and is gone by morning. Any brawler with bad hands gets called Lanz sooner or later, and the archive has not confirmed a single sighting.
