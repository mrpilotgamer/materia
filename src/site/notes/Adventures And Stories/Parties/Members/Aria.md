# Aria

Aria, daughter of the Archangel Samiel, was the sorceress of [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Retribution\|Retribution]]. She was born in a small town in the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Empire of Bornumia]], half human and half celestial. Her father is the Angel of Time, one of the great celestials who rank below the Progenitor Angels, and he keeps his home on [[Places/Planes/Outer Planes/Seven Mounting Heavens of Celestia\|Celestia]].

## In Retribution

Aria's magic was born in her, carried in her father's blood. She worked many spheres, above all Warp, and she was a master of wild magic, which most mages avoid for good reason.

She was cautious and kept somewhat apart, even within the party. She was also curious, and once she set herself to something she saw it through.

The archive credits the banishment of [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Rouge, the Red Progenitor Demon\|Rouge]] to the party as a whole, but stories name Aria as the one who performed the ritual in the faceless emperor's palace. No one who was there has said so.



## After the War

Her father's blood has kept her alive long past a human span. After the war she settled in Sireth, the floating capital of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imnas\|Imnas]], and raised a family there. Around 5367 she left the city, and she has not been seen in public since. She is still living, but nobody knows where. The archive tried to find her for this entry and failed. Its letters came back unopened, and those who went looking found only places she used to live.
