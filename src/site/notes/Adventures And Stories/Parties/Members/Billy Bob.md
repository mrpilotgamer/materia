# Billy Bob

Billy Bob of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Laurland\|Laurland]] was the healer of [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Retribution\|Retribution]]. He was [[Species and Cultures/Races/Vullukk\|Vullukk]], the fourth generation of the Bob family to carry the name, and by every account an unusually gifted healer.

## The Bob Family

The first Bob came to Laurland from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]] on a trading ship and stayed. Laurland is a [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Dali\|Dali]] island, where families take Dali names, but the Bobs kept their own out of plain stubbornness, and in Felavik they are known for it.

The family keeps two traditions. Every Bob has a lifelong obsession with one food or flavor, and Billy's was chocolate, a rare luxury grown in the hot south of the island. And every Bob, on coming of age, leaves home on a journey to prove themselves. Billy's journey took him to [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]], where he met the rest of the party.

## In Retribution

Billy Bob healed through the Life sphere, on his own talent, and answered to no temple. Those who knew him remember him as a kind man.

Stories say he could bring back the dead, and that doing so brought him face to face with [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Wathoria, God of Equity\|Wathoria]], the god of death and Judge of Souls. The archive cannot confirm either.

## After the War

He went home to Laurland when the war was over and carried on the family guild, Rogues Are Us. The Bobs' rogues work much like locksmiths, though their trade runs wider: they open what is locked and find what has been lost, for honest pay and always within the law. Billy ran the guild for the rest of his life, and he died in time, with a bar of chocolate in his hand.
