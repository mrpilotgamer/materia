# Vera

Vera, Chaos Lord of the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Empire of Nox]], is the only member of [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Retribution\|Retribution]] whose later life the archive can follow. She is Daemedi, born in Adriacium during the Faceless Century, and she lived most of her life there before she left and fell in with the party in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]].

## In Retribution

Vera stood at the front. She was famously hard to kill, and stories say she came back even from death more than once, though no witness has come forward. She fought through the war of 5267 and was there when the party banished [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Rouge, the Red Progenitor Demon\|Rouge]] in the faceless emperor's palace.

## Chaos Lord

At some point during the war Vera stopped being mortal. No one knows what caused it. She is now something the world has no proper name for, a new kind of lesser deific being, far from the power of a demon lord. "Chaos Lord" is the stand-in title, taken from her chaotic nature and her devotion to freedom.

She raised [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Nox]] from [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Empire of Adriacium\|Adriacium]]'s ruins in 5267 and has stood at its head ever since, though she leaves the governing to the Council of Seven. She spends the first seven months of each year in Nox and the last seven off the Material Plane. Where she goes is not known.

She has little patience for flattery and less for statues, and the [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Algar\|Algar]] know better than to offer her either.
