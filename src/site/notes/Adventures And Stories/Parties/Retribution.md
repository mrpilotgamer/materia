# Retribution

Retribution was the party of four who banished [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Rouge, the Red Progenitor Demon\|Rouge]] from [[Places/Materia\|Materia]] in 5267 and ended the war that brought down the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Empire of Adriacium\|Empire of Adriacium]]. Few events in the archive matter more, and few are recorded so thinly. The accounts that survive are patchy, and what follows is what they agree on.

### Party Members

[[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Members/Vera\|Vera]] - Daemedi of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Empire of Adriacium\|Adriacium]], the party's shield, now Chaos Lord of the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Empire of Nox]]

[[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Members/Billy Bob\|Billy Bob]] - Vullukk healer of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Laurland\|Laurland]]

[[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Members/Aria\|Aria]] - Half-celestial sorceress of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Bornumia]], daughter of the Archangel Samiel

[[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Members/Lanz\|Lanz]] - Human fighter of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Empire of Adriacium\|Adriacium]], who fought with his bare hands

### Synopsis

The four came together in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]], though none of them was born there. Vera and Lanz had both left Adriacium during the Faceless Century, Aria came from Bornumia, and Billy Bob from Laurland. How they met is not recorded.

Somewhere along the way they learned that the faceless emperor of Adriacium was Rouge himself. They kept it to themselves. When his army of demons and devils marched out of Adriacium in 5267, Retribution fought it there, and then crossed the ocean to press the danger on the powers that would listen. [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/The Rayhostian Theocracy\|The Rayhostian Theocracy]] answered first and sent soldiers. [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imnas\|Imnas]] sent battle mages, and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Pimestalia\|Pimestalia]] sent a single advisor, Seki Thundersword. The party had no connections in [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imestalia\|Imestalia]] or [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Yoichizu\|Yoichizu]] and asked nothing of them.

The reinforcements halted the army inside Bornumia. Retribution went back into Adriacium and performed a ritual in the faceless emperor's palace at Lincugus that banished Rouge from Materia. The army broke apart without him. The palace has been sealed ever since, and the Phylakes of Nox guard it.

Only after the war did the party say who the faceless emperor had been. By then most of the world preferred to believe it had been a demon incursion gone wrong, and the claim has passed into myth.

### After the War

Vera stayed. Somewhere in the war she had stopped being mortal, though no one knows how, and she raised the Empire of Nox from Adriacium's ruins in the same year. She rules it still.

Billy Bob went home to Laurland and died there in time. Aria is still alive, carried well past a human span by her father's blood, though the archive does not know where she is. Lanz vanished. Stories place him all over Alora, and none of them can be checked.
