The Progenitors are some of the oldest beings in the multiverse, defined by their strong beliefs. There are fourteen of them. Seven are tied to the upper planes and seven to the lower planes, and each holds the strongest domain on its plane. They share their seven colors with the [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials\|Primordials]].

The archive calls the upper Progenitors "Angels" and the lower ones "Demons". These are the archive's own terms for which side of the planes a Progenitor belongs to. They do not mean a Progenitor is a celestial or fiend of any particular kind, and a "Progenitor Demon" may rule a plane of devils or yugoloths.

## List of Progenitor Demons

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Azure, the Blue Progenitor Demon\|Azure, the Blue Progenitor Demon]] - The Mistress Manipulator. Any lie is fine if it gets you ahead, and an ally is only an ally until someone better comes along.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Blanc, the White Progenitor Demon\|Blanc, the White Progenitor Demon]] - The emptiness at the heart of life. Nothing means anything, so the only honest thing to do is be alone.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Jaune, the Yellow Progenitor Demon\|Jaune, the Yellow Progenitor Demon]] - Breaker of Minds. She uses fear and madness to break anyone who stands against her.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Noir, the Black Progenitor Demon\|Noir, the Black Progenitor Demon]] - Lord of the Nine and Leader of the Strong. The strong are meant to rule the weak, and power belongs to whoever takes it.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Rouge, the Red Progenitor Demon\|Rouge, the Red Progenitor Demon]] - Breaker of Chains. Fight for what you love, and let nothing hold you back.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Vert, the Green Progenitor Demon\|Vert, the Green Progenitor Demon]] - Lady of Envy. Whatever you want badly enough is already yours, so take it.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Viole, the Purple Progenitor Demon\|Viole, the Purple Progenitor Demon]] - Lord of Nobility. Some are born to rule, and the world belongs to those bold enough to reach for it.

## List of Progenitor Angels

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Angels/Kollane, the Yellow Progenitor Angel\|Kollane, the Yellow Progenitor Angel]] - The Ever-Smiling. Fear and sadness are selfish, and his followers are never allowed either.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Angels/Lilla, the Purple Progenitor Angel\|Lilla, the Purple Progenitor Angel]] - Voice of the Many. Her followers give up their own thoughts and think as one.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Angels/Must, the Black Progenitor Angel\|Must, the Black Progenitor Angel]] - Servant of Order. Obey rightful authority completely, and want nothing for yourself.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Angels/Punane, the Red Progenitor Angel\|Punane, the Red Progenitor Angel]] - The Eternal Martyr. Spend your life on other people's causes, and die for them gladly.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Angels/Roheline, the Green Progenitor Angel\|Roheline, the Green Progenitor Angel]] - The Infinite Giver. Owning anything is theft, so give until you have nothing left.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Angels/Sinine, the Blue Progenitor Angel\|Sinine, the Blue Progenitor Angel]] - Speaker of All Truths. Every secret is selfish, and nothing may be hidden.

[[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Angels/Valge, the White Progenitor Angel\|Valge, the White Progenitor Angel]] - Validator of Souls. An empty life can be filled with meaning through selfless work.
