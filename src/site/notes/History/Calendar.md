Years on [[Places/Materia\|Materia]] are counted from the end of the [[History/Great Schism War\|Great Schism War]]. A year written as 5437 PC means the 5437th year Post-Cataclysm. Nothing before the war can be dated, and the archive files everything from that time under [[History/Ages/Prehistory\|Prehistory]].

## The Year

A year has 14 months of 28 days each, 392 days in all. The year opens on the 1st of Dillimar, the first day of winter.

The week has seven days, each named for one of the [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials\|Primordials]]:

| Day | Named for |
|---|---|
| Kilia | [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Ki, Yellow Primordial Spirit\|Ki]] |
| Shiria | [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Shiroi, White Primordial Spirit\|Shiroi]] |
| Kuria | [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Kuro, Black Primordial Spirit\|Kuro]] |
| Akia | [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Aka, Red Primordial Spirit\|Aka]] |
| Aoia | [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Aoi, Blue Primordial Spirit\|Aoi]] |
| Murasia | [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Murasakino, Purple Primordial Spirit\|Murasakino]] |
| Midoria | [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Midori, Green Primordial Spirit\|Midori]] |

A month is exactly four weeks, so every month begins on Kilia and ends on Midoria, and a date falls on the same day of the week every year.

## Seasons

Each season lasts 98 days, three and a half months. The solstices and equinoxes fall in the middle of their seasons.

| Season | Begins | Midpoint |
|---|---|---|
| Winter | 1 Dillimar | Winter solstice, 21 Mergar |
| Spring | 15 Arlaxara | Spring equinox, 7 Arlaya |
| Summer | 1 Wathriam | Summer solstice, 21 Maltiam |
| Autumn | 15 Coptiama | Autumn equinox, 7 Malbia |

Materia has no moon. Its night sky holds only stars.

Two festivals have no fixed date. [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Aka, Red Primordial Spirit\|Aka]]'s First Clearing is held on the first hard frost of autumn, and [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Aoi, Blue Primordial Spirit\|Aoi]]'s First Storm on the first great storm after a calm.

Some cultures keep their own reckoning alongside the common one. The [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Dulandi\|Dulandi]] open their year with *Calan Gaeaf* on the first day of winter, which falls on 1 Dillimar. The [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Algar\|Algar]] see the old year out with the *Eleutheria*, three days in Lincugus as one year ends and the next begins.
