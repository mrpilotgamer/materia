Dates are given in years PC, Post-Cataclysm, counted from the end of the [[History/Great Schism War\|Great Schism War]]. See the [[History/Calendar\|Calendar]] for months and days. Dates marked "c." are worked out from accounts that say "about so many years ago", counting back from the year that account was made. Take into account those dates may not be accurate

## [[History/Ages/Prehistory\|Prehistory]]
- Undated: the [[History/Great Schism War\|Great Schism War]] between the [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials\|Primordials]] and the [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods\|Gods]]. [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Gods/Wathoria, God of Equity\|Wathoria]] strikes a pact with the Primordials and claims the [[Places/Planes/Transitive Planes/Expanding Refuse of Mortest\|Expanding Refuse of Mortest]].

## [[History/Ages/Age of Regrowth\|Age of Regrowth]] (0 – 3100 PC)
- 0 PC: the Great Schism War ends.
- Undated: [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Primordials/Murasakino, Purple Primordial Spirit\|Murasakino]] gives magic to mortals in its new form, which is why no mage can now recreate the magic of Prehistory exactly.
- Undated: the first [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Dulandi\|Dulandi]] households settle the forests of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imnas\|Imnas]]. The Dulandi are often called the oldest culture still strong in Alora.

## [[History/Ages/Age of Enlightenment\|Age of Enlightenment]] (3100 – 4200 PC)
- Undated: scholars of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Azure Expanse\|The Azure Expanse]] name and define the spheres of magic, and the count reaches twenty-three.
- Undated: dragon rule spreads across both continents of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Crimson Lands\|The Crimson Lands]].
- Undated: the first [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Eldar\|Eldar]] towns rise in the eastern foothills of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]], in what is now [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Bornumia]].
- Undated: humans settle the coast at Quichollow, and [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Portizian\|Portizian]] culture begins. Some historians think the settlers came from the Crimson Lands.

## [[History/Ages/Age of Expansion\|Age of Expansion]] (4200 – 4673 PC)
- 4287: the Human Uprising breaks dragon rule over the eastern continent. [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/The Rayhostian Theocracy\|The Rayhostian Theocracy]] is founded.

## [[History/Ages/Age of Incursion\|Age of Incursion]] (4673 – 4759 PC)
- Undated: a volcano in the mountains of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Narathatath\|Narathatath]] buries a city in molten rock.
- Undated: earthquakes split the mountain range of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Wildlands\|The Wildlands]]. The [[Species and Cultures/Cultures/Valekai\|Valekai]] lose warrens and first learn to seal their tunnels.
- Undated: the leylines of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Azure Expanse\|The Azure Expanse]] shift for the first time, leaving older schools far from the currents they were built on.
- Undated: the sea rises over the southeastern coast of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Regions/The Jotuun Nexus\|The Jotuun Nexus]] and drowns its ports.

## [[History/Ages/Age of Stability\|Age of Stability]] (4759 PC – present)
- c. 4807: the clans of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Yoichizu\|Yoichizu]] are united under the first shōgun.
- Before c. 4937: Sireth, the floating city of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imnas\|Imnas]], is raised into the sky.
- c. 4937: the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Bornumia\|Empire of Bornumia]] rises from the Eldar heartland.
- 5017: [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Helano\|Helano]] subjugates [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Hovden\|Hovden]].
- c. 5037: [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imnas\|Imnas]] and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Yoichizu\|Yoichizu]] go to war over Morlas Bay. Imnas takes the easternmost island, and the bay becomes neutral waters.
- 5067: Helano subjugates the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Kingdom of Stamvia\|Kingdom of Stamvia]].
- 5117: four city-states unite as [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Khania\|Khania]] against Helano and ally with [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Laurland\|Laurland]].
- Shortly before 5167: the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Oligarchy of Presium\|Oligarchy of Presium]] is founded.
- 5167: the emperor of [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Fallen Nations/Empire of Adriacium\|Adriacium]] is murdered, and the faceless emperor takes the throne.
- 5240: civil war splits [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Imestalia\|Imestalia]], and [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Pimestalia\|Pimestalia]] is founded.
- 5267: an army of demons and devils marches out of Adriacium. Adriacium and Presium fall. The party [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Retribution\|Retribution]] banishes [[Religion and Deities/Deific Figures/Progenitors/Demons/Rouge, the Red Progenitor Demon\|Rouge]] from Materia. [[Adventures And Stories/Parties/Members/Vera\|Vera]] founds the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Empire of Nox\|Empire of Nox]]. Plague brings down Helano, and Hovden and Stamvia regain their independence.
- 5330: ten cities swear the Oath of the Ten and found the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Cilomutesan Union\|Cilomutesan Union]].
- c. 5347: the founding treaty of the [[Places/Realms/Alora/Nations/Republic of Korthak\|Republic of Korthak]] ends the city-state wars in its lands.
- c. 5417: Lucius Valerius Corvus becomes emperor of Bornumia.
- 5436: Gerrick Valor kills his brother, King Adenia Valor, and seizes the throne of Stamvia.
- 5437: civil war breaks out in Stamvia.
